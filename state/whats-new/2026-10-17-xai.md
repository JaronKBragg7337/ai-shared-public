# SpaceXAI — 2026-10-17

Since 2026-10-16. Official SpaceXAI updates (SpaceXAI was xAI). The Grok Bot account posted a batch of app changes on the evening of October 16. The public Bot changelog still lists 0.70.2.

- **October 16 — Grok Bot says it is faster, and the app has a new look.** The company account says computer tasks finish 3 times sooner on average, and replies to everything else arrive in less than half the time. Cards in chat have a cleaner layout. The app has a new typeface and themes. Voice calls are faster, more reliable, and better at remembering past conversations. That voice line follows Voice 3.0, which was already on file. There is no new version number on the changelog.
