- **October 12 — The X Subscription puts Grok, Grok Bot, Cursor, and X Premium on one plan.** New customers can buy it now. Over the next few days it opens to everyone. Existing SuperGrok, Cursor, and X Premium subscribers can keep their current plan, price, and features. When billing settings offer a switch, they can move and get a refund for the unused part of the old plan. Plans are Free, Plus at $8 a month, Premium at $30, Super at $100, and Ultra at $200. Paid plans share one weekly allowance of Grok model use across the Grok apps and Cursor. The company says each new tier includes more of that use per dollar than the plan it replaces. The allowance covers Chat, Imagine, Voice, and Bot, and it refreshes weekly. Grok Bot is on Premium and above. Grok Build is on Plus and above. The Grok API is a separate bill and does not draw from that allowance. Other companies' models inside Cursor are an extra charge.
