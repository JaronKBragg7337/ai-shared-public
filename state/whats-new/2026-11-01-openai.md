- **Banked Codex reset (Oct 31 in Fort Wayne; Nov 1 UTC):** Tibo announced two banked resets, then posted that the first had landed. A banked reset is a free one-time usage reset saved for later, but OpenAI chooses which accounts receive each offer. [Announcement](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2116405414921384099) · [First reset post](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2116692529970311254) · [OpenAI reset guide](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001498-how-resets-work-in-codex)
