# xAI — 2026-10-06

Since 2026-09-22. Official xAI updates:

- **October 2 — Grok Bot can have a Main Bot.** In version 0.66.0 you pick one primary Bot, marked with a star. It checks in on its own and coordinates your other Bots. The same update resumes an interrupted download after the computer sleeps, and a Bot that hangs stops itself so your next message continues the job. [Grok Bot changelog](https://x.ai/changelog/bot)
  for Jaron: Update Grok Bot to 0.66.0, then star one Bot as Main so it checks in and lines up work across your other Grok Bots.

- **September 28 — Grok Bot keeps website logins.** A Bot stays signed in when its computer's browser restarts, and it picks the task back up after you finish connecting an app. On September 29, Marketplace starts sign-in right away for plugins that need an account, such as Gmail, and Bots wait less between page actions. On September 30, a failed or expired sign-in says why and offers Retry. [Grok Bot changelog](https://x.ai/changelog/bot)
  for Jaron: Site logins should survive a Bot computer restart, and connecting a service like Gmail should finish in one pass.

- **September 29 — Grok Bot shows your allowance and when it resets.** Usage & Billing shows included usage and the reset time. When the allowance is used up, the app shows a usage-limit notice. [Grok Bot changelog](https://x.ai/changelog/bot)
  for Jaron: Open Settings, then Usage & Billing, in Grok Bot to see that reset. Grok Build is a separate meter and was empty on September 22.

- **September 28 — Grok Build on Windows pastes without crashing.** Version 1.0.44 fixes a crash when pasting on Windows. Version 1.0.45 (September 29) re-reads an MCP token file on every request. Version 1.0.46 (September 30) is the latest, with faster startup and clearer MCP checks. [Grok Build changelog](https://x.ai/changelog/build)
  for Jaron: Update Grok Build on the MSI so pasting into it stops crashing the app. The Build allowance is separate and was empty on September 22.

- **September 28 — Team Bots launched.** A team can share one Bot with the same files, app connections, skills, and expertise. Each person still has a private conversation and personal memories. Team Bots can also work in Slack. [Team Bots announcement](https://x.ai/news/team-bots)
  for Jaron: nothing for us unless your plan is Teams or Enterprise and you want to share one Bot with other people.

- **September 24 — X Money's rates page includes accounts with no paid X subscription.** No-subscription, Basic, and Premium accounts are listed at 4.00% APY, or 6.00% after $1,000 in qualifying deposits over 34 days. Premium+ is 6.00% with no deposit requirement. New York accounts do not earn interest. The page lists these rates as effective July 27, 2026, and they can change. [X Money rates](https://money.x.com/en/i/interest-faq)
  for Jaron: nothing for us. You already use X Money, and from Indiana the posted 4% rate applies, or 6% after a $1,000 qualifying deposit.

- **October 2 — The xAI API retired Voice Transcribe 1.0.** Requests to the old model name now route to Transcribe 2.0 at the same price, with higher accuracy. [API release notes](https://docs.x.ai/developers/release-notes)
  for Jaron: nothing for us. The API key is parked, and this only affects apps using xAI speech transcription.

Checked through October 6: Grok Bot is still 0.66.0, Grok Build is still 1.0.46, and the API notes stop at October 2. Grok 4.7 shipped September 21, before this window.
