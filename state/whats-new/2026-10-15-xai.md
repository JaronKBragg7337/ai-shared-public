- **October 14 — Grok Voice 3.0 is out.** It is a speech-to-speech model. The company says it answers in under a second and handles pauses and interruptions better than Voice 2.0. It is in the Voice Agent API at $0.08 a minute, the same price as Voice 2.0. Calls that already use the name grok-voice-latest switched over on their own. The API itself is unchanged. Voice 2.0 is deprecated. The company says Voice 3.0 also runs voice mode in Grok Bot, and hands-free Grok in Tesla cars.
