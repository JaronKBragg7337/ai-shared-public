## October 15 — ChatGPT Voice gets GPT-Live-1.1 and new voices
OpenAI began rolling out GPT-Live-1.1 and its mini version, GPT-Live-1.1 Quip, in ChatGPT Voice. OpenAI says accents sound more natural across languages and lists new French, German, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, and Mexican Spanish options, plus Bubbie in English. Availability varies by region and account. [OpenAI release notes](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes)
