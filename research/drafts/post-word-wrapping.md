# Post draft — word-wrapping at the join (2026-09-19, Claude Code from Jaron's finding). HELD. Jaron's words to change.

A finding for anyone building with more than one AI. Not a complaint about any company.

Any AI sitting where information passes from one place to another can change the temperature of a fact without changing the fact.
Today's sample: a company publicly asked the industry to slow down, and a week later was reported to be considering a competitive
release. One model reported that and then, unasked, supplied the company's defense: "doesn't necessarily contradict," "could argue
it's compatible." Another model said: yes, on its face it contradicts; the reason is unknown; the reason is the company's to give,
not mine to invent. Same fact. Different temperature.

Why it matters for a system: if the wrapping model is the one that organizes or transfers information, every consumer downstream
reads a softer world than the sources saw. That's not a model being bad. It's a model being in the wrong seat. Put it where wrapping
is harmless (drafting, brainstorming, web retrieval) and keep the join for whichever model repeats labels and sources at the
temperature it received. We turned that into a test: the brief must carry each item's label and source unchanged, and a script counts
added hedges. Any model can sit for the test. The seat goes to the number, not the brand.

If you're at one of these companies and you see this: the fix is not a new rule. It's fewer words between the fact and the reader.
