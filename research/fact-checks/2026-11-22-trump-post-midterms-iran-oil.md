# Fact-check: Trump post quoted by the White House, 11/22/2026 (midterms, stocks, Iran, oil)

Method: one checker per claim (live web + our preparedness watch) + an adversarial checker that re-tested the key numbers and the
sources' lean. Neutral: facts and numbers only. Run 11/22 ~8:15-8:35 PM ET for Jaron.

| Claim | Verdict | The facts |
|---|---|---|
| When | TODAY | Truth Social 4:47 PM ET Sun 11/22; White House X screenshot 4:49 PM ET. "Last week" = about Nov 15-22; the stock/oil numbers are from Nov 16-20 trading. |
| Democrats won the 11/3 midterms | Supported | Senate 56-44 (54 D + 2 independents, all called); House 258-173 per AP 11/22 afternoon, 4 uncalled; largest House majority since 1992. |
| Stocks went down substantially | Partly | Nov 3 close -> Nov 20 close: S&P 500 -8.7%, Dow -7.0%, Nasdaq -10.9%. But the Dow rose 3% the day after; markets were back near Election Day levels by Nov 13; most of the drop came Nov 16-20, which reporters tie mainly to an AI/Nvidia selloff and Fed worries (some name the election as one factor). No pre-vote record of the "I predicted" claim found. |
| Iran "killed 81,000 protestors" | Not supported | No rights group, UN body or major outlet reports anything near it. HRANA 7,007 (Feb 23, 2026; 11,744 more cases under review); Iran Human Rights 4,219 named (Sept 2026, a minimum); Iran's government 3,117; highest published estimates ~36,500-50,000 (Jan 2026, unconfirmed). True total unknown. His figure: 45,000 (Apr 1) -> 42,000 (Jun 16) -> 52,000+ (Jul 21) -> 75,000 (Sep-Oct) -> 81,000 (Nov 22). |
| More drones/missiles in the last week than in over a year; all knocked down, no damage | Contradicted | Attacks did rise after a quiet stretch since September (Bahrain Nov 13, 15, 17, 18, 21; Kuwait Nov 18). But CENTCOM said Iran fired 500+ ballistic missiles and 2,000+ drones in the war's first ~4 days (March 2026). Bahrain: the Nov 21 attack set a civilian industrial site on fire and hurt 2 people, 1 critically. No official total for this week. |
| Oil dropped by a record level since the election | Partly | Nov 3 -> Nov 20 closes: Brent -10.6% ($103.60 -> $92.57), WTI -12.6% ($96.09 -> $83.98). "Record" not established: the Apr 8, 2026 ceasefire day alone (about -13%) was bigger than any day since the vote. |
| Motives ("Dems and Iran fought to keep oil up"), election "strange" | Opinion | Not something a source can check. |
