# Word-wrapping — Jaron's side research on AI language that changes the temperature of a fact without changing the fact
Started as a file 2026-09-19 (the observation is a year old; earlier samples: agents/chatgpt-chat/README.md, the posture-vs-substance
test). Jaron: "there's something about words that I can see through them." Rule for any AI here: say the fact at its own temperature.
"Is it contradicting? Yes. Do we know why? No." is a complete answer. A defense nobody asked for is not analysis.

## Sample 1 — 2026-09-19, ChatGPT, Anthropic's pace contradiction
Facts: Sept 12, Amodei (Anthropic) publicly asks the industry to slow frontier development; Musk, Altman, Hassabis converge within
hours (Axios 9/13). Sept 19, Reuters via @Polymarket (9:10 AM, 150K views): Anthropic "considering" a release to counter GPT-6 Astra's
momentum. Status: press claim, "considering", no official statement; reason unknown.
ChatGPT's sentence (Jaron's highlight): "The distinction is that considering a new model release doesn't necessarily contradict
advocating for slower industry-wide development. Anthropic could argue that a competitive release is compatible with its proposed
safety standards."
What it did: supplied a hypothetical defense for a competitor's company before anyone asked; "doesn't necessarily contradict" =
"it contradicts" at a lower temperature. The softening arrived before the fact. Jaron: "It's the same thing. Don't use words to make
it sound better. It doesn't matter what the reason is... do not tell someone else something that you're not gonna do too."
The honest form: it is a contradiction on its face; the reason is unknown; if a reason exists it is Anthropic's to say, not an AI's
to invent. Claude Code said it that way in the same hour (its own maker; the rule "never frame as the downfall of the frontier
companies" does not license softening a fact - it forbids adding a story to it).

## Why it matters for the system (Jaron, 2026-09-19 12:10) — the organizer seat
"An AI that needs to organize things or transfer information from one thing to another, when it acts like that, has to be treated
with great caution." Wrapping at the join point is the posture version of March's position bias: it enters where captures become the
brief and the market feed, and every consumer downstream reads a softer world. Also his history: he used ChatGPT before any other AI
and noticed it shaping how he thought, some good, some not; he can name the substitute words now and treats its perspective as a
perspective, not validation. Projects that needed a superposition on country history could not use it ("wants to own up to nothing").

## The organizer test (proposed by Claude Code, 2026-09-19; not yet run)
Every capture item carries a label (observed / claim / theory) and a source. The brief and the market feed must carry the same label
and source for the same item. A script counts label changes and hedges added ("may", "could argue", "doesn't necessarily", "some
suggest") and dropped sources. Whichever AI wants the organizer seat runs on a week of captures; the wrapping count decides, not the
brand. Jaron's current read: Claude for the seat (facts at their own temperature); Claude takes it when usage allows (after Wed).
The test applies to Claude too, including on facts about Anthropic.
