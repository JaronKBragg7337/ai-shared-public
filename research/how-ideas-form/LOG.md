# How ideas form (spark log)
Purpose (Jaron 11/16): capture the moments right before ideas connect, so other people's AI teams can learn the *way* ideas get connected, not Jaron's conclusions. The principles ledger (projects/principles/LEDGER.md) is the main record: many principles were born from moments like these, with the example kept alongside. This log only adds what the ledger leaves out (what came right BEFORE the connection) and points to the P-number. Not every principle needs an entry; some are just game-design rules. One entry per spark. "Don't know what triggered it" is a valid answer.

Format: date | trigger (what came right before) | the connection | analogy | other paths considered | what came of it | where it went next (same method reused elsewhere) | correction (if an AI misread it and Jaron fixed it)
