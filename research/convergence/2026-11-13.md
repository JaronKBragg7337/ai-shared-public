# Convergence 2026-11-13

## China and Taiwan

Taiwan's Defense Ministry recorded 30 PLA aircraft sorties, 8 navy ships, and 3 official ships from 6 a.m. 12 November to 6 a.m. 13 November (UTC+8). It said 23 sorties crossed the median line into the northern, central, southwestern, and eastern ADIZ. Taiwan sent aircraft, ships, and coastal missiles. Taiwan News said the month's running total is now 174 aircraft and 130 ships.

Reuters reported on 13 November that a senior Taiwan security official, who was not named, said China's navy and coast guard massed more than 100 vessels in the western Pacific and around Taiwan early this month. The official said the drills included simulated attacks on other militaries and on cities, including Taiwan's, and that the deployment was larger than Justice Mission 2025. Two Taiwan security officials told Reuters that about 70 vessels were still active on Friday. Joseph Wu wrote on 12 November that the total was down, and that groups from the Northern, Eastern, and Southern Theater Commands plus the Liaoning carrier group were still operating east of the Philippines. The same unnamed official told Reuters that a Chinese submarine launched an intercontinental ballistic missile from the Yellow Sea into the Chinese mainland during the drills. That is one official's account. China's defense ministry did not answer Reuters. Beijing has described the carrier's western Pacific training as routine and not aimed at any country. Taipei calls the unnamed drills "silent intimidation," meant to pressure governments before next week's APEC summit in Shenzhen, where President Trump and President Xi are expected to meet. A foreign intelligence source told Reuters the lack of a public name was meant to apply military pressure and avoid a diplomatic backlash.

## Russia and Ukraine

Ukraine's Air Force said that from 6 p.m. on 12 November Russia launched 117 strike drones, 63 of them jet-powered Shaheds, plus Gerbera and Parody decoys, from Oryol, Kursk, Millerovo, Shatalovo, Primorsko-Akhtarsk, and occupied Crimea. As of 7:30 a.m. on 13 November it said 80 had been shot down or suppressed, with hits at 24 places and debris at 10 more, and that some drones were still airborne. Local authorities told the Kyiv Independent that attacks over the past day killed 9 people and injured 61. Donetsk governor Vadym Filashkin said 4 were killed, 3 of them in Kramatorsk. Odesa governor Oleh Kiper said one strike injured 4 at a furniture plant and a later strike on a residential building killed 2 and injured 5. Zaporizhzhia governor Ivan Fedorov said a 17-year-old was killed. A drone set fire to a hospital in Kyiv's Shevchenkivskyi district, injuring 3, with about 30 people evacuated. A later Kyiv Independent report said a further strike on Zaporizhzhia injured 30 people, including 6 children.

Russia's Defense Ministry claimed it destroyed 1,198 Ukrainian drones between 8 p.m. Thursday and 8 a.m. Friday Moscow time, over 16 regions, the Moscow region, Crimea, and the Black Sea. That figure is a Russian claim. Volgograd governor Andrei Bocharov said 4 workers were killed and 2 injured in the city's southern industrial zone. He did not name the site. The Moscow Times, citing Astra, and Ukrainian channels identified Lukoil's Volgograd refinery from photographs. AP reported that Ukraine's General Staff said it hit that refinery and started a fire. Moscow mayor Sergei Sobyanin said 467 drones were headed for the Moscow region and that 144 were stopped as they approached the capital.

Ukraine's General Staff reported 175 engagements on 12 November, 13 of them on the Kostiantynivka sector and 11 on Pokrovsk, plus 1 missile strike, 67 airstrikes, and 232 guided bombs. It estimated 2,590 Russian personnel losses in that day. Those are Ukrainian figures.

On Thursday night at Baldonnel, outside Dublin, President Zelensky said his meeting with Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner produced the largest prisoner exchange of the war, and that Europeans would join the next round with their own voice. Kushner said the exchange was finalized and would return thousands of people. Neither side published a number or a date. Kremlin aide Yuri Ushakov said the track would continue, that another contact was expected before a Putin-Trump meeting at APEC in China, and that the swap parameters were still being agreed. He did not answer whether Europe would sit at the table.

President Zelensky said on 13 November that a speed limit on Starshield over occupied territory and the front makes it impossible to use interceptors against Shahed-type drones, and he told the foreign and defense ministries to press the United States. Interfax, citing Defense Ministry and Armed Forces specialists, said the detected cap is 161 km/h. That is a Ukrainian account of a US system. No US confirmation was published today.

## Iran war

Iran's Revolutionary Guards navy said a tanker carrying liquefied natural gas struck a naval mine and exploded in the Strait of Hormuz on Friday while using what they called an "unauthorised and dangerous route." State media carried the claim. Reuters had no ship name and no further details.

The Guards said they struck a US early-warning radar in Bahrain, and described it as retaliation for an air attack on a telecommunications tower in Sirik and as a warning over aerial attacks and oil shipping in the strait. US Central Command said the Guards launched two one-way attack drones toward Bahrain and that US forces shot both down. CENTCOM said there were no injuries and no damage. The two accounts are both on the record. They do not describe the same result. Al Jazeera said this is the first Iranian claim of an attack on US military assets in a Gulf country since early September.

The IAEA, in a confidential report seen by Reuters, said it is "increasingly concerned" that Iran has not let it inspect the Isfahan Fuel Enrichment Plant, which Iran declared on 12 June 2025. The agency said it does not know the plant's exact location, its safeguards status, or whether it holds nuclear material, and that the missing information is a serious proliferation concern that needs to be fixed with urgency. Iran told the agency the situation will not be resolved until "the root causes" are addressed. Inspectors visited Bushehr in September and have not returned to the other bombed sites.

No new sighting of USS Theodore Roosevelt was published on 13 November. It was last reported through the Strait of Malacca on 8 November with its tracker off. A US official had said the group would not enter Central Command's area before mid-November.
