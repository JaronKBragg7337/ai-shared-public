## China and Taiwan

Taiwan's Defense Ministry recorded two PLA counts on 12 November. From 6 a.m. 11 November to 6 a.m. 12 November (UTC+8), its air force page listed 7 aircraft sorties, 6 navy ships, and 2 official ships, with 3 sorties in the southwestern and eastern ADIZ. The ministry's English post and Taiwan News listed 7 navy ships rather than 6. From 9:38 a.m. local time, the ministry reported 29 sorties, including J-10, J-16, JH-7, KJ-500, and Y-20U aircraft, and said 22 crossed the median line into the northern, central, southwestern, and eastern ADIZ for joint air-sea training. Taiwan sent aircraft, ships, and coastal missiles. On 11 November, Foreign Minister Wang Yi called Secretary of State Marco Rubio, at China's request, about President Trump's trip to the APEC leaders' meeting in Shenzhen on 18-19 November and President Xi's trip to the G20 in Florida. The US readout says they agreed that leader-level talks matter for "peace and denuclearization," including any trilateral meeting with Russia. China's readout called the call positive and constructive and did not mention Russia, denuclearization, or Taiwan. This air activity follows Defense Minister Wellington Koo's 10 November statement that the Liaoning group and other formations had run undeclared "area denial" drills in the Western Pacific. South Korean press, citing the Wall Street Journal, reported on 12 November that US lawmakers are pushing three chip-control bills into the defense authorization bill.

## Russia and Ukraine

Russia attacked Ukraine overnight on 11-12 November with Iskander-M/KN-23/S-400 ballistic missiles and Zircon anti-ship missiles, plus 123 drones, 73 of them jet-powered Shahed-type, the Ukrainian Air Force said. It did not give a missile count. As of 8 a.m., Ukraine said it had shot down or suppressed 5 Banderol/Dan-T drones and 89 other drones, with impacts at 17 places. The Kyiv city administration said 1 person was killed and 7 injured in the capital. Local authorities told the Kyiv Independent that strikes over the past day killed 10 people and injured 40, including at least 4 killed at a market in Chornomorsk. Russia's Defense Ministry claimed hits on a rocket factory in Kyiv, a drone workshop and a missile-fuel factory in the Kyiv region, and substations in Zhytomyr. Moscow's mayor claimed 5 Ukrainian drones were downed heading for the capital, and the ministry claimed 71 across Russia. Ukraine's General Staff reported 167 engagements on 11 November, 14 of them on the Kostiantynivka sector. US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner met President Putin in Moscow on 12 November, their arrival following two days of US-mediated talks in Abu Dhabi that produced a planned large prisoner exchange and no public deal on territory. The Kremlin said the Moscow talks were useful and that the US side backed a Russian proposal for a joint humanitarian mission to Kostiantynivka. Putin said any European troops in Ukraine after a ceasefire would be legitimate targets and the war would resume at once, and that NATO could not expand further. Russian state media reported that Witkoff said a new trilateral round was agreed and that Europeans would join it. President Zelensky, in Ireland, said there could be a window to end the war before 2027 and that he was still sceptical.

## Iran war

Iran's Revolutionary Guards said two supertankers carrying more than 4 million barrels of oil were disabled by explosions overnight on 11-12 November on a southern route through the Strait of Hormuz that Tehran calls unauthorized. Iranian state media said the ships hit naval mines. A later army statement, reported by Reuters, said two more tankers hit mines on Thursday afternoon in the eastern strait and caught fire. Reuters said it could not verify the accounts. A 12 November check cited by Streamline found no matching new entry at UK Maritime Trade Operations, whose latest logged incidents were dated 10 November. The Guards called the fires the result of "blind trust" in the US military. That is an Iranian claim. Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf said on 11 November that the strait will not return to its prewar status. In Saudi Arabia, civil defense said debris from two Houthi ballistic missiles aimed at the Makkah region fell on Sabuhah village after the missiles were intercepted, with the intercepts reported the evening of 11 November. President Erdogan, after meeting President Masoud Pezeshkian in Antalya on 12 November, said diplomacy to end the US-Iran war should resume without further delay. The Security Council is set to hold an open Gulf briefing on 13 November at Bahrain's request. USS Theodore Roosevelt is still on the Pacific-to-Gulf leg: it left Singapore after a 6 November port call, crossed the Strait of Malacca around 8 November with its tracker off, and a US official told Axios it would not enter Central Command's area before mid-November. It is expected to join USS George H.W. Bush and the Japan-based USS George Washington, both already on the Iran blockade. National Interest reported that status on 10 November.

China's readout does not mention Russia, denuclearization, or Iran.
