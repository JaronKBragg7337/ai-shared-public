# Convergence 2026-11-16

## China and Taiwan

Taiwan's Defense Ministry recorded 4 PLA aircraft sorties, 7 navy ships, and 3 official ships from 6 a.m. Sunday to 6 a.m. Monday (UTC+8). It said all 4 sorties crossed the median line into the central, southwestern, and eastern ADIZ. Taiwan sent aircraft, ships, and coastal missiles. Taiwan News said the month's running total is now 205 aircraft and 146 ships. That daily air count is up from 1 sortie in the previous window and still far below 30 on 13 November. The ship count around the island dropped by one. No new public total for the wider unannounced Pacific deployment was published today. On Friday, two Taiwan security officials told Reuters about 70 vessels were still active after an early-November peak an unnamed official put above 100. Taipei calls those drills "silent intimidation" ahead of APEC in Shenzhen. Beijing has called the carrier training routine and not aimed at any country.

China's Foreign Ministry confirmed Monday that Xi Jinping will meet Donald Trump and Vladimir Putin in Shenzhen on Thursday, 19 November, on the sidelines of APEC. Spokeswoman Mao Ning said the three will hold "in-depth exchanges on safeguarding world peace and promoting strategic stability in major-power relations" and called the meeting "of ground-breaking significance." She did not name Iran, Ukraine, or nuclear cuts.

The US Commerce Department's Bureau of Industry and Security issued an interim final rule on Monday, written at the direction of Treasury and the National Security Council, to stop remote access to advanced American computing chips and servers through data centers in third countries. BIS said the rule is meant to keep existing restrictions from being bypassed. Reuters said it could curb China's access and noted that it comes as Trump leaves for the Asia trip that includes the meeting with Xi. Forbes and CBS, citing the department, said the rule adds a license requirement for exports, re-exports, or transfers of advanced chips to Brazil, Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, and Thailand. TechCrunch described the remote-access rules as also covering the US arms-embargo group, which includes China, Russia, and Iran. That is TechCrunch's reading of the covered list. BIS's own release does not name those countries. Trump said Monday, "Selling the other chips is a good thing, not a bad thing," drawing a line under chips the existing controls do not cover. On Sunday, Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said the United States does not need new chip-sale legislation, only enforcement. Yesterday's note recorded tighter AI-chip rules as still under consideration. A rule is now issued.

## Russia and Ukraine

Ukraine's General Staff reported 141 combat engagements on 15 November, 18 of them on the Kostiantynivka axis, plus 94 airstrikes, 282 guided bombs, 7,429 kamikaze drones, and 2,337 shelling attacks, 18 of them with multiple-launch rockets. It said Ukrainian aircraft and artillery struck two Russian troop concentrations and one drone control post. It estimated 2,560 Russian personnel losses that day. Those are Ukrainian figures.

Ukraine's Air Force said Russia launched 158 long-range attack drones overnight on 16 November, 77 of them jet-powered. By about 8 a.m. it said 131 had been intercepted, 55 of them jet-powered, with drones still airborne, strikes at 19 places, and debris at 11. The Energy Ministry said overnight strikes cut power in Vinnytsia, Dnipropetrovsk, Kirovohrad, Odesa, Ternopil, and Cherkasy. The Kyiv Independent, compiling local authorities for 15-16 November, put the civilian toll at 6 killed and 45 injured. That count includes 3 killed in Druzhkivka and 1 in Lvivka in Donetsk, 1 killed and 13 injured in a morning strike on a road in Dnipro, and 1 killed and 4 injured in Zaporizhzhia. Kyiv had more than 20 air-raid alerts in the period and reported no casualties in the capital. A separate dpa tally of Monday's exchanges said Russian strikes killed at least 3 people in Ukraine. The two counts cover different windows.

Ukraine's General Staff said it struck the Ilsky refinery in Krasnodar Krai, hitting three primary processing units and the gasoline complex, and an oil depot in Azov in Rostov region. It called both sites short fuel routes to Russian forces on the front and in occupied Crimea. Damage at Ilsky was still being assessed. These are Ukrainian military claims. Rostov governor Yuri Slyusar said Ukrainian drones started fires at four industrial sites in Azov and that three men were hospitalized. Krasnodar authorities reported a fire at a company site in Ilsky and said six people were injured in Severskaya when debris hit a house. dpa said Ukrainian drone attacks killed two people in Russia. Russia's Defense Ministry said it intercepted 1,336 Ukrainian drones on Monday and called that the largest such claim since the full-scale invasion. That figure is a Russian claim. PBS said US-mediated talks have had no effect on the fighting and that Kushner and Witkoff expect more discussions with no date set.

The Institute for the Study of War's 15 November assessment said Russian forces are using the Starshield speed limits to strike Ukrainian energy sites, and that Ukrainian forces recently advanced south of Novopavlivka. That is ISW's reading. Zelensky's complaint that a speed cap is letting jet-powered drones through was already on the record Sunday. No US confirmation of a policy cap was published today.

RFI reported Monday, citing unnamed sources, that French military personnel have been in Ukraine since at least summer 2026 on a confidential mission called Volia, training troops and maintaining French-supplied equipment, in short rotations rather than a permanent garrison. The number was not disclosed. A source told AFP that French personnel are operating in Ukraine and have no combat role. The French General Staff did not comment. RFI said French lawmakers had not been formally informed.

Estonia summoned a Russian diplomat on Monday over what it calls a Russian sabotage attempt against a drone manufacturer. Foreign Minister Margus Tsahkna said Putin failed to reach his war aims in Ukraine and is trying to sow uncertainty among Ukraine's allies. ISW's 15 November assessment said Estonia would close its border with Russia after an attempted Russian arson attack. Those are Estonian and ISW accounts of Russian activity on NATO territory. Belarusian Security Council State Secretary Alexander Volfovich said Alexander Lukashenko ordered an unscheduled combat-readiness check of border-guard rapid-response units.

Nuclear safety regulators from 38 countries warned Monday that the risk of a nuclear accident in Ukraine is rising because Russian attacks threaten the power grid, and they called on Russia to stop those attacks. The EU finalized 113 new Russia sanctions listings, including almost 50 banks, refineries, the St. Petersburg oil terminal, shadow-fleet ships, and oil buyers in China, the United Arab Emirates, the Seychelles, and Oman. A separate decision listed 30 people and two entities tied to Russian disinformation and cyber activity. The EU also disbursed 1.7 billion euros for Ukrainian drones, ammunition, and air defense. A Pentagon inspector general report, reported by Stars and Stripes, found that support for Ukraine is straining US Army readiness in Europe.

## Iran war

US officials told the Associated Press, Axios, the Wall Street Journal, Al Jazeera, and The National that USS Theodore Roosevelt entered US Central Command's area on Sunday, 15 November. Stars and Stripes said CENTCOM had not answered a request to confirm the arrival. The reported strike group is Carrier Air Wing 11, the cruiser USS Chosin, and the destroyers USS Paul Hamilton and USS Decatur. The same officials say Roosevelt joins USS George H.W. Bush, in the region since April, and USS George Washington, in the region since August. Stars and Stripes said it is not clear whether all three will stay or whether Roosevelt will relieve one of them. This is the second time this year US officials have described three carriers in the Middle East. The carrier was last publicly placed in the Strait of Malacca around 8 November. Yesterday's note said no arrival in Central Command had been confirmed.

The IRGC said it launched six drones on Sunday at a US air-defense radar at Bahrain's Riffa base and claimed a successful hit. Bahrain said its air defenses intercepted and destroyed several Iranian aerial attacks before they reached their targets. Those accounts still do not describe the same result. Stars and Stripes noted that Riffa is about 11 miles from Naval Support Activity Bahrain, the base of US Fifth Fleet.

The IRGC said two more tankers exploded in the Strait of Hormuz on Monday and that six vessels have caught fire and sunk in the strait since Sunday. Iran's navy said a tanker on what Tehran calls an unauthorised route, with navigation systems off, hit a mine and caught fire. These are Iranian claims. They do not name the ships or their flags. No independent identification of a sunk ship was published today. The same pattern of unnamed "mine" fires was claimed on 12, 13, 14, and 15 November. The US military says it has cleared the main routes of mines. Separately, UK Maritime Trade Operations said it received a report of a vessel hit by an unknown projectile while leaving the Strait of Hormuz on Monday morning. It reported a fire and no casualties, and it did not name the ship. It is not established that this is one of the vessels Iran described. Euronews reported that oil prices rose Monday after the incidents.

Euronews also reported that the US military struck Iranian missile launchers at Bandar Lengeh and Sirik on Sunday night. No CENTCOM announcement of those strikes was found. A GlobalSecurity campaign log for 15 November said no US strike on Iran was announced in that window. Treat the Bandar Lengeh and Sirik strikes as an unverified press claim.

The Wall Street Journal, cited by Just Security, reported that US officials estimate Iran still has 1,500 to 2,500 missiles, and that the estimate is low-confidence because the stockpile is hard to count. That figure sits against Trump's repeated claim that the United States has knocked out much of Iran's military. Both are claims. Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said Monday that Iran remains committed to diplomacy. Al Jazeera reported Iranian warnings that Tehran may strike before another US attack. Those warnings are Iranian claims.

## Links today

All three capitals now say Trump, Xi, and Putin meet in Shenzhen on Thursday. They still do not describe the same meeting.

Beijing confirmed the date on Monday. Mao Ning's agenda line is "world peace" and "strategic stability." She did not mention Iran or Ukraine. Trump told reporters on Sunday, "We'll talk a lot of things, including denuclearisation, but we'll be talking a lot of things. We're trying to end the war in Iran." AFP reported that US officials say he is not expected to raise Ukraine in the trilateral. Kremlin aide Yuri Ushakov told reporters the meeting will be at the Chinese leader's official residence and should be structured in the spirit of what he called the wartime alliance of the three countries and their role in creating the United Nations. He said it should have a significant impact on the international situation. He did not, in that Reuters account, put Ukraine or Iran on the trilateral agenda. Ushakov also said a separate Putin-Trump meeting will happen because both will be in Shenzhen, and that the time and place are still being set. On 12 November he said that bilateral would continue discussion of a Ukraine settlement. The US line, the Chinese line, and the Russian line are all claims. They agree on the day and the city. They do not agree on the subject.

Ukraine's side of that disagreement is also on the record. AFP reported that President Zelensky has warned the meeting could become a Yalta-style division of spheres of influence, and that Foreign Minister Andriy Sybiha said a US-China-Russia meeting would "only encourage Putin to continue this war." German President Frank-Walter Steinmeier said Xi showed irritation when Ukraine was raised and said Europeans must be at the table. UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres said Monday that a real commitment by the three to denuclearisation would be a great achievement. Beijing has long said Washington and Moscow should cut their arsenals before China joins such talks.

Two sourced contacts tie the Iran war to the other capitals on the same day. Russia's Foreign Ministry said Sergei Lavrov spoke with Abbas Araghchi on Monday about "the current crisis in the Middle East resulting from the unprovoked US and Israeli aggression against Iran." Iranian foreign-ministry sources, carried by Mehr, described the same call as consultation on political efforts to preserve peace. The Jerusalem Post reported the Roosevelt's arrival as happening as Trump prepares to discuss Iran with Xi and Putin. That is a sourced pairing of the carrier's move into Central Command with the summit. It is not evidence that the carrier was timed to a Chinese or Russian military move.

The EU's new Russia sanctions name oil buyers in China. That is a sanctions link between the Ukraine war and Chinese firms, reported by the Kyiv Independent from the listings finalized in Brussels on Monday. It is not a statement that Beijing directed those buyers, and it is not a military link.

The BIS remote-access rule is aimed at the China chip path and was issued the day Trump left for the Xi meeting. TechCrunch says the covered group also includes Russia and Iran. BIS's release does not say the rule was written to connect the three wars.

No source today tied the 4 PLA sorties, the 158-drone night, or the Hormuz claims to one another. No source said the PLA count was timed to the Roosevelt's arrival or to the Shenzhen meeting. The Pentagon inspector general's finding that Ukraine support strains US Army readiness in Europe was published the same day as the carrier report. No source connected those two facts.
