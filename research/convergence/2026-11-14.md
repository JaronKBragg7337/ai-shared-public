## China and Taiwan

Taiwan's Defense Ministry recorded 10 PLA aircraft sorties, 8 navy ships, and 3 official ships from 6 a.m. Friday to 6 a.m. Saturday (UTC+8). It said 4 sorties crossed the median line into the central, southwestern, and eastern ADIZ. Taiwan sent aircraft, ships, and coastal missiles. Taiwan News said the month's running total is now 184 aircraft and 141 ships. That daily air count is down from 30 sorties in the previous window. No new public total for the wider unannounced Pacific deployment was published today. On Friday, two Taiwan security officials told Reuters about 70 vessels were still active, after an early-November peak the unnamed official put above 100. Taipei calls those drills "silent intimidation" ahead of APEC in Shenzhen. Beijing has called the carrier training routine and not aimed at any country.

On the same trip, a senior US official told reporters, including the Jerusalem Post, that China is not delivering rare earths at the pace Washington wants, and that the administration is considering tighter rules on exports of advanced American AI chips. The same official said the Nvidia scandal should not be part of the talks with Xi. Those are US statements about policy still under consideration, not a new control that has been issued.

## Russia and Ukraine

Ukraine's Air Force said that overnight on 13-14 November Russia launched 96 attack drones, 61 of them jet-powered Shaheds, plus Gerbera drones and Parody decoys, from Bryansk, Oryol, Primorsko-Akhtarsk, occupied Donetsk, and Hvardiiske in Crimea. As of 7:30 a.m. Kyiv time it said 81 had been shot down or suppressed, with hits at 8 places and debris at 2 more, and that some drones were still airborne. RBC-Ukraine reported that the casualty count from Friday's strike on two apartment blocks in Zaporizhzhia rose to 50, including 8 children, and that a rescuer was killed when a slab fell during the search. Anadolu, citing local authorities, said a drone strike on Saturday killed a woman in Yasna Polyana in the Kramatorsk district, a morning strike on Hlukhiv in Sumy injured 3, the Kharkiv region reported 1 killed and 2 injured over the past day, and 2 people were injured in Dnipropetrovsk. Odesa governor Oleh Kiper said a morning strike on a DTEK facility in the north of the region cut power to nearly 50,000 people. Belgorod's operational headquarters said a Ukrainian drone hit a house in Proletarsky and killed a married couple and their 24-year-old daughter, and that 7 more people were injured in other attacks in the region. Russia's Defense Ministry said its overnight strikes hit an energy site in Chernihiv region and a dry cargo ship it said was carrying military cargo toward Odesa. The ministry's target list is a Russian claim.

The Institute for the Study of War's 13 November assessment, posted overnight, said Ukrainian forces recently advanced east of Slovyansk and south of Novopavlivka. ISW also said Moscow will accept European officials in the talks only for a new European security architecture, and that it still wants Europe to meet Russia's 2021 demand to dismantle NATO. That is ISW's reading.

On the talks themselves, the claims diverge. Jared Kushner said Russia had agreed to include Europe so negotiators can take up postwar European security and the territorial issues together. European Council President Antonio Costa said a team from the UK, France, Germany, Italy, Poland, and the EU will be sent. Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov said Russia can decide on an expanded format only once Europe's "true intentions" are clear. German Chancellor Friedrich Merz said, "Russia wants to talk about questions of European security architecture. We don't need to talk about that. There is one," meaning NATO. EU foreign policy chief Kaja Kallas said Europe should insist on a ceasefire first, and that taking up Russia's security demands would walk into a trap.

## Iran war

Iran's navy and Revolutionary Guards said on Saturday that fires broke out on three oil tankers after they struck naval mines in the Strait of Hormuz. The Guards said two tankers, one of them a supertanker, had switched off radar and positioning and were using what Tehran calls an unauthorised route in the south of the strait. The regular navy said a third tanker, also with tracking off, caught fire and stopped in what it called an unsafe and prohibited zone east of the strait. Neither statement gave a name or a flag. These are Iranian claims. AFP reported them. The US military says it has cleared the main routes through the waterway of mines. No independent identification of the three ships was published today. The same pattern of unnamed "mine" fires was claimed on 12 and 13 November.

No new public position for USS Theodore Roosevelt was published today. It was last reported through the Strait of Malacca around 8 November with its tracker off. A US official said the group would not enter Central Command's area before mid-November. Today is that window, and no arrival has been confirmed.

## Links today

Washington and Moscow both now put Trump, Xi, and Putin in Shenzhen on 19 November, and they do not describe the same meeting.

The White House said on Friday night, in briefings reported Saturday, that the trilateral will close Trump's China visit, after his bilateral with Xi on Thursday afternoon. Spokeswoman Olivia Wales said he will discuss cutting nuclear arsenals. A senior US official said he intends to raise the Iran war in that room, and will not discuss the war in Ukraine there, because "China is not really a part of that track." The official said Washington expects no agreement and called the session "the entry point, the first conversation." The Jerusalem Post, which attended the briefing, also reported that a senior official said no separate Trump-Putin bilateral is scheduled on this visit. Musk is expected on part of the China stop and will not be in the trilateral or the Xi bilateral.

On Saturday, Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov told RIA Novosti that an agreement has been reached for a three-way meeting of Putin, Xi, and Trump on 19 November in Shenzhen, on the sidelines of APEC. He did not give an agenda in that statement. On 12 November, Kremlin aide Yuri Ushakov said the planned Putin-Trump meeting at the same summit would continue discussion of the full range of issues in a Ukraine settlement. The US line and the two Russian lines are all claims. They do not agree on whether Ukraine is in the room, or on whether there is a separate Trump-Putin meeting.

Beijing has not matched either account today. BBC, reporting the White House briefing, said Chinese state media had noted that Xi would hold bilateral talks at APEC and had not reported a trilateral, and that Moscow and Beijing had not yet commented when that story was filed. The last Chinese statement on the three-leader format remains Mao Ning's 4 November line that China is in communication with the relevant parties. No Chinese confirmation of a 19 November date, or of an Iran agenda, was found today.

ISW's 13 November China-Taiwan update, and a Reuters commentary the same day, assessed that the unannounced drills rehearse isolating Taiwan while the United States is tied up with the Middle East war and Ukraine. That is an analyst judgment. Neither Beijing nor the PLA has said the drills were timed to the other two wars.

No source today tied the 10 sorties, the 96-drone night, or the three Hormuz mine claims to one another. No source tied today's PLA count to the Theodore Roosevelt's transit.
