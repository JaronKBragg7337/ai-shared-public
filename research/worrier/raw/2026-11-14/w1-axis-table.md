## Shapes I noticed

**1. Iran is working Moscow through its security council, and the tempo jumped around the US midterms.**
Lavrov in Tehran (Oct 19) -> Pezeshkian with Putin (Oct 27) -> Rezaei-Putin phone call -> alleged Russian envoy to Khamenei (days before Nov 3) -> Rezaei in the Kremlin with Khamenei's letter (Nov 5) -> Zakharova repeats the uranium offer (Nov 11).
- Lavrov -> Pezeshkian -> Rezaei: **CAUSAL CHAIN** (each visit follows up the last; Rezaei explicitly briefed Putin on the Oct 26 Tehran meeting).
- The cluster as a whole and the US midterms (Nov 3): **SHARED CAUSE** (Al Jazeera ties the diplomacy to fear the US renews strikes after the vote).
- Rezaei's visit (Nov 5) and the FT launcher story (Nov 5-6): **SAME TIME ONLY**. The documents describe a 2024 order.
- Rezaei's visit / FT story and Task Force Iron Gate adding the Caspian (Nov 7): **SAME TIME ONLY**, possibly SHARED CAUSE (long-running US concern about the Russia-Iran Caspian corridor). No source links them.

**2. China runs a parallel Iran track, one level lower and never at the top.**
Araghchi in Beijing (Sep 16) -> Bagheri Kani in Beijing the same day as Oman's Sultan (Nov 2) -> Zhai Jun in Iran (Nov 8). Xi reportedly avoided a formal sit-down with Pezeshkian in Bishkek.
- These three and APEC hosting: **SHARED CAUSE** (Beijing wants Hormuz calm and its summit clean).
- Bagheri Kani in Beijing (Nov 2) and Rezaei in Moscow (Nov 5): **SHARED CAUSE** (same Iranian body sending its No. 2 and No. 1 to the two capitals inside four days).

**3. Iran's security council has taken over the outside channels from the foreign minister.**
Rezaei hosts the Saudi/Pakistani/Turkish visitors, goes to Putin, and is the person Vance reportedly asked to meet; his deputy goes to Wang Yi. Araghchi's last solo big-power meeting found was Sep 16. This is a pattern in who shows up, not a sourced claim about Iranian politics.
