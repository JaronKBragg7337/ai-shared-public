**Connections**

- Cuba's collapse and the Iran war: **SHARED CAUSE, partly.** The blockade is a direct US choice, not a side effect of the war. But the war makes it bite harder: diesel is scarce and dear worldwide, and Russia's spare diesel is now promised to the US (see section 7) instead of Havana. *Wrong if:* Russian or Mexican cargoes start arriving in Cuba despite the tight market, which would show supply was never the limit - only US pressure.
- Trump's Cuba deadline and the midterm losses / stalled Iran war: **SAME TIME ONLY.** Analysts quoted by Newsweek say a weakened president may want a quick "win"; that is opinion. *Wrong if:* no force movement follows (no carrier or amphibious group shifts toward the Florida Straits, no evacuation notice, no airspace warning) - then it was talk.
- Venezuela's opening (Exxon, IDB, Doha) and the Hormuz closure: **SAME TIME ONLY.** It is tempting to read it as "replace Gulf barrels with Venezuelan ones", but no source I opened says so. *Wrong if:* the Exxon terms and US licenses turn out to predate the war or carry no export-volume targets.
