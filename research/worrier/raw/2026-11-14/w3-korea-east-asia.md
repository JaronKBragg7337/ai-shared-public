## Top 3 worries from this region

### 1. A shooting incident on the inter-Korean land border
- **Evidence:** Three soldiers maimed by mines (Sept 21) and a formal armistice-violation finding. About 50 line crossings in Aug-Sept, dozens more in mid-October, 200+ warning-shot occasions since August by the North's own count. South Korean rules allow direct fire if troops push more than 50 m south after a warning shot. Kim Yo Jong says new firepower units are in firing positions and talks of "pre-emptive" measures. The two armies disagree on where the line is in the eastern sector. Every offer of talks (military talks, medical aid, the UN chief's appeal) has been refused. No working channel.
- **Labels:** incidents ESTABLISHED; "firepower units in position" CLAIMED; intent to escalate NOT ESTABLISHED (Seoul's own read is construction crews under deadline pressure).
- **What would prove it wrong:** crossings and warning shots dropping off as winter stops construction; the North taking up border-line talks; UN Command general-officer talks actually happening; several quiet weeks.

### 2. A bigger North Korean step in the weeks after APEC, paid for with a record theft
