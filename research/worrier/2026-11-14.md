That anyone is timing a move to it: NOT. *Wrong if:* a carrier returns to Japan, Korean border crossings stop for the winter, inspectors get back into Iran, and North Korea's year-end meeting passes with no new weapons order.

## 3. Patterns I see

**1. Link: **SHARED CAUSE** (the calendar). A joint plan: **NOT**. *Wrong if:* the reporting on restraint holds through December.

**2.
