## Touches our plans
- [16] Small business / banking: FinCEN proposed a rule on customer due diligence. Banks would verify an LLC's beneficial owners when the account first opens (not at every new account), the beneficial-owner info they must collect would be spelled out, and an ITIN would no longer count in place of an SSN. Stage: proposed Nov 17; comments close 60 days after Federal Register publication (verified on treasury.gov). Why it matters: it covers what a bank asks for when Heartbeat Observatory LLC opens its business account. https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0691/
