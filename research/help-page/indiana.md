## 1. Replacing a lost ID or birth certificate

### Indiana state ID card (BMV)
- Official page: https://secure.in.gov/bmv/licenses-permits-ids/learners-permits-and-drivers-licenses-overview/identification-cards/ (VERIFIED)
- Fee: $9 for new, renewal, replacement or amended ID. FREE if you're 18+, a US citizen, and eligible to vote (state law IC 9-24-16-10). Also free for youth 16+ under DCS care and homeless youth with a fee-and-consent waiver affidavit. (VERIFIED)
- Bring originals or certified copies: 1 proof of identity (e.g. certified US birth certificate), 1 proof of lawful status, 1 proof of Social Security number, 2 proofs of Indiana residency. (VERIFIED)
- No address? Fill out the Indiana Residency Affidavit (https://forms.in.gov/download.aspx?id=10390) and bring a letter on letterhead from a government agency or 501(c)(3) nonprofit (shelter, etc.) with its name, address, phone, and a legal representative's name, signature and date, saying they serve you and accept mail for you. Real ID details: http://www.realid.in.gov/bmv/fees-taxes/home (VERIFIED)
- Living in a vehicle or staying with a friend: other affidavit options on the same form (VERIFIED).

### Indiana birth certificate (Indiana Dept. of Health, Vital Records)
- Official page: https://www.in.gov/health/vital-records/ (VERIFIED)
- Fee: $10 search fee includes one certified copy; $4 each extra copy in the same order; non-refundable. (VERIFIED) Local county health departments can set different fees (UNVERIFIED for Allen and St. Joseph counties; check).
- Fastest: walk-up at the local health department in the county where you were born. State office has no walk-in. (VERIFIED)
- Other ways: mail (form https://forms.in.gov/download.aspx?id=5623, check or money order), phone 24/7 <phone>, or online via VitalChek. State office questions: <phone>. State processing about 10-15 business days after receipt. (VERIFIED)
- ID is required; mail orders need a copy of front and back of photo ID, and the mailing address must match the ID. (VERIFIED) What to do if you have no ID at all is NOT clear on the state page; ask the local health department. (GAP)
- No statewide fee waiver for homeless people found. (GAP; shelters/caseworkers often help pay, UNVERIFIED)
- Born in another state: order from that state; CDC list "Where to Write for Vital Records".

## 3. SNAP (food stamps) and Medicaid (FSSA / Division of Family Resources)
- Apply online: FSSA Benefits Portal https://fssabenefits.in.gov/bp/ ("Apply Online for SNAP/Cash Assistance" or "Apply Online for Health Coverage"); also a screening tool to check eligibility. (VERIFIED)
- By phone (including Medicaid applications): <phone>, free language help. (VERIFIED)
- In person: local DFR office in every county, Mon-Fri 8 AM-4:30 PM. Office finder: https://www.in.gov/fssa/dfr/ebt-hoosier-works-card/find-my-local-dfr-office/ (VERIFIED)
- Mail documents: FSSA Document Center, P.O. Box 1810, Marion, IN 46952. (VERIFIED)
- Medicaid decision can take up to 90 days; free certified navigators help. https://www.in.gov/medicaid/members/apply-for-medicaid/ (VERIFIED)

## 4. Phone programs (Lifeline)
- Lifeline is a federal discount of up to $9.25/month on phone or internet (not itself a free phone). Qualify by income at or below 135% of federal poverty guidelines, or by getting SNAP, Medicaid, SSI, and some other programs. (summary of FCC/USAC pages)
- Apply: USAC National Verifier https://www.usac.org/lifeline/national-verifier/ , FCC info https://www.fcc.gov/lifeline-consumers , Indiana OUCC page https://www.in.gov/oucc/telecommunications/tips-and-publications/lifeline-telephone-assistance/
- Then pick a participating carrier; some give a free phone and plan (e.g. Assurance Wireless <phone>, listed in the Fort Wayne Rescue Mission guide; UNVERIFIED current offer).
