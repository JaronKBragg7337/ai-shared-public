## What was built: `heartbeat-observatory/lab/pet/` (Jaron's 11/3 idea)
A pet made of soft cells. Not an AI, no talking. It wanders, naps, eats the berries you drop, and grows a new cell
every 3 berries (24 cells at hatch, cap 110 for the phone). Phone-first at 375 px.

| file | what |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | the page (three 0.160.0 from jsdelivr, same as the other lab pages) |
| `world.js` | grass patch, props (pebbles, mushrooms, stump with a lantern, a name sign), sky, day-night by the phone's clock, camera, touch, name card, save |
| `pet.js` | **the module**: `createPet(scene, options)` for a loft or a farm later; one InstancedMesh body (one draw call), fresnel rim + inner glow jelly shader, eyes that blink and look at food / your finger / you, smile, blush, berries |
| `pet-core.js` | the body and the life with no three.js in it: cells + springs + repulsion, hops, growth (buds + settles), hunger, idle/wander/sleep/eat/follow/poked, save shape |
| `README.md` | the module API and how it works |
| `test/pet-sim.test.mjs` | node test |
| `test/browser-check.mjs` | headless Chromium check at 375 px with a local three.js copy |

Controls: tap grass = berry; tap pet = squish + happy wobble; hold = it follows the finger; two fingers = orbit + pinch.
Save: localStorage `hb-pet:v1` (name, colour, cells, bites, hunger, born time), try/catch. First run: name + colour (6).
Hungry = droopy (flatter, slower, half-lidded). Night = lantern, fireflies, stronger inner glow, more naps.

## Tests (all in the cloud container)
- `node lab/pet/test/pet-sim.test.mjs`: **40/40.** Ten simulated minutes with berries, pokes, a pretend finger and a
  quiet night: no NaN, no cell over the speed cap, no cell off the body, never through the floor, 75 berries eaten,
  25 cells grown (24 -> 51), naps happened, size cap holds at 110 and a full-size pet is stable (0.08 ms per frame
  in node). Save/load round-trips; a 5 h absence makes it peckish, not starving; junk and tampered saves are handled.
- `node lab/pet/test/browser-check.mjs`: **43/43** in headless Chromium (375x667 @2x day and night, 1100x700 dusk),
  offline with a local three copy: hatch screen, name + colour saved, berries eaten, grew after 3 bites, poke, hold-to-
  follow moved it, no sideways scroll, card + gear fit, 32-50 draw calls, no page errors. Screenshots looked at by me:
  it reads as one cute creature, face visible, grass soft, night cozy.
- `node --check` passes on pet-core.js, pet.js, world.js.
