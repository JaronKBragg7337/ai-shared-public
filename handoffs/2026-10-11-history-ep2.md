## Verified live (web, 10/11) and from where
- Snow 1855 text (UCLA epi-snow site, MSU Matrix archive): 500+ fatal attacks in 10 days within 250 yards; 83 deaths examined, 10 nearer another pump, 5 families used Broad St, 3 schoolchildren; workhouse 535 inmates, 5 deaths, own well (Summers/others give 530); brewery owner Mr. Huggins, 70+ workmen, none severe, malt liquor; widow 59, died Sept 2, water fetched Aug 31, niece in Islington also died; Board of Guardians Sept 7, handle removed "following day"; daily attacks 143 (Sept 1), 116, 54, 12 (Sept 8), 11, 5, 5, 1.
- Grand Experiment table (Snow, reproduced in several textbooks): 315 / 37 / 59 per 10,000 houses and the house/death counts above. 315/37 = 8.5, so the screen says "about eight times". Wikipedia says "14 times" (different figures); I did not use it.
- 1855 committee rejection (secondary: Wikipedia Miasma theory, historical review), Farr 1866 (same), Pacini 1854 (Wikipedia/PubMed), Koch 1883 (sources say 1883 in Egypt, announcement Jan 1884; screen says 1883 and "1883-84" in sources).

## Disputed / not established, handled on screen or here
- The handle: "removed" is Snow's own wording. Linda Hall calls the popular story unverified; the Survey of London says a vestry committee chained the handle in 1855 and it was later unchained. Screen shows Snow's account and flags how much it mattered as DISPUTED. The narration says "parish Board of Guardians" (Snow's words); Wikipedia/another source says the vestry. Left as Snow wrote it.
- Daily attacks for Sept 4-7 and Aug 31 are not in the text I could read (the table is an image). The chart shows only verified days and says "4-7 not shown". Fix by reading Snow's Table I image if we want a full chart.
- Map is SCHEMATIC: street layout and dot positions are invented placeholders (clustered around the pump), labelled as such on screen. I did not have Snow's coordinates. A real redraw needs his georeferenced death data (public datasets exist).
- Hampstead widow: details from Snow's text via UCLA; one account names her Susannah Eley, I did not use a name.
- "Each served rich and poor" is Snow's phrase. Not used: "same streets/intermingled pipes", "300,000 people", "Lambeth moved intake in 1852" (not confirmed in my reads), seven-week period.
- Farr 1866 and the 1855 committee come from a secondary source (Wikipedia, citing the committee text); worth a primary check before YouTube.
