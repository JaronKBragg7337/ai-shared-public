# YouTube Shorts cuts — Terra, 2026-11-11

- Added `tools/make_short_cuts.py` for queued portrait videos. It uses the opening, looks for a silence between 55 and 75 seconds near 60, skips existing outputs, and verifies output before naming it. It supports `--dry-run` and `--id SHOW_ID`. FFmpeg/FFprobe are preferred; PyAV and NumPy provide a fallback when those executables are unavailable.
- Made 26 `-short.mp4` files next to the original videos; none of the original show files were changed. The three Keeper's Light social sources are 42 seconds each and were not padded or cut: the requested 55–75 seconds is longer than the originals. Those originals already fit the requested Shorts duration, but whether their existing YouTube posts appear as Shorts was not checked.

Existing YouTube uploads are backfilled by upload date, at most one successful backfill per Pacific day; a missing or failed cut remains retryable and does not block later candidates.
