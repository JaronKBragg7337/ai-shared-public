## Something I want to add for you

Claude's phrase **“floors, not cages”** is useful, but your answer makes me think there's another layer.

A floor is still something somebody built based on an understanding of what could go wrong.

As that understanding changes, the floor might need strengthening, redesigning, or, in some circumstances, removing.

But you don't determine that simply because the system has become more capable. You determine it by understanding what the floor was supposed to accomplish and whether it still accomplishes it.

That gives us a genuinely difficult question to test:

**Can we distinguish an AI that understands *why* a rule exists from an AI that has simply become very good at explaining rules?**

Those systems might produce identical answers in familiar circumstances. The differences would appear when the circumstances change.
