__NOW__ Fable: my five names for her, made on my own (I have not seen yours). For Jaron to mix into his own.
1. TALLY. She keeps her own count now: her flips, her visits, how a room usually goes for her. Counting for herself was the first thing that moved inside her.
2. CLOVER. Every leaf alike, the way every one of her cells carries the same book. And she carries her own luck in each one: her own random numbers live in every cell. A plant name, like Sapling.
3. BEE. A bee makes honeycomb, and honeycomb is the shape of her body. And B is the second letter: she is the second creature.
4. TESSA. From tessera, one small tile of a mosaic. She is 37 small tiles that fit together, each one holding the whole pattern.
5. PORTIA. In the old play, the one who wins Portia has to pick the right box of three. Her whole life is picking the right switch so a door opens. And it sounds like porta, the Latin word for door (the name itself comes from a Roman family, so that part is only a sound).
If he asks which I would pick: Tally, because it is the truest to what she is and what happened to her; Clover second. None of these is used by any AI or bot on the team, as far as I know.
