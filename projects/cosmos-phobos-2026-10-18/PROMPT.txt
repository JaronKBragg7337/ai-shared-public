Goal: reaching, landing on and walking on Phobos must look right and work on an iPhone.

2. In the game, go to Phobos. Check: it can be reached, landed on and walked on; Mars looks large in the sky; low gravity feels right; nothing floats, pops or clips; the player isn't stuck. Take screenshots (save under games/the-cosmos/docs/qa/phobos-2026-10-18/).
3. Fix what you safely can. Real terrain and real scale only - nothing faked. Don't touch other games or pages.
4. Re-run the phone check until it passes. Bump games/the-cosmos/build.json id if the game's own rules say to.
5. git add only files under games/the-cosmos/ and commit with a clear message. Do NOT push (the runner pushes only if the phone check passes).
Write a plain-English report to games/the-cosmos/docs/qa/phobos-2026-10-18/REPORT.md: what was wrong, what you fixed, what you could not fix, and which screenshots show it.
