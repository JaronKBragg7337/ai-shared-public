# Local giveaways for what people already do (Jaron, 2026-10-10 5:12 PM, driving past Halloween houses)

## The idea
Reward people for things they're ALREADY doing - start with Halloween decorations. Walk up: "I love your decorations - we're
running a giveaway, best decorations by <date> wins $150-200 (maybe $500 later). OK if we photograph your house? We cut the
address out." ~20 houses; each tells neighbors -> trees where one person seeds the next. Free to enter, nobody pays.

## Why it works (his reasoning)
- His music years: people watched videos for the LIFESTYLE shown, not just hype.
- MrBeast: giveaways - the chance of being one of those people is slim, like the lottery, but you watch to see others win and
  hope it's your day.
- Don't start with games/gimmicks; reward natural behavior. Paying products grow networks too, but a no-cost way to take part
  and maybe win may be the seed for getting the foundation known. Possibly the investment that's needed.

## Claude notes (for when it's built)
- Legal shape: a JUDGED contest (best decorations = skill) with NO entry cost is not a lottery (lottery = prize + chance + paid
  entry). Write simple official rules: dates, who judges, how picked, prize paid by Heartbeat Observatory LLC. Prizes over $600
  to one person in a year can need a 1099 - stay under or collect a W-9.
- Consent: photo permission from the owner each time; blur house numbers, street signs, plates, faces of kids.
- Content: every visit = a short video (house, owner's reaction, the win) for TikTok/X/YouTube + site + TV - boost-safe (no trading).
- Money: from the system's profit later; a first $150-200 test is small enough to try this season.
- Seasons: Halloween now; Christmas lights next (bigger, Dec); spring yards/gardens, summer cars, etc.
