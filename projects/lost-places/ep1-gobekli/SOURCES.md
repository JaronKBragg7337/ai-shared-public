## Date and interpretation limits

The requested 11,600–9,500 years is a popular broad age description. The episode uses the explicitly published settlement bracket c. 9500–8000 cal BCE (Clare 2020), approximately 11,500–10,000 years ago, rather than implying an exact 9,500-year-old endpoint or one date for all monuments. “Before farming” is qualified as before a fully developed farming economy: cultivation of wild plants is not ruled out. The opening familiar sequence is deliberately labelled incomplete; villages need not postdate farming. Drawings show generic pillars, animals and layouts, not surveyed reconstructions, readable ancient writing, or a documented roof arrangement.
