# Growth model - Jaron's words, 2026-10-10 9:14 PM (business strategy, not personal philosophy)
- No growth by beefing or tearing others down. Grow by helping people build their own networks; what they create draws interest.
- Charge for ENTERTAINMENT and wants, never for NEEDS. Needs get traded, given time for, or simply made available - no stress.
- "Abundance" won't be all butterflies: some things can be abundant because other things pay for them. That's the model.
- COVID as data: when everyone was stuck inside, creators who were nobodies blew up, streamers of 10+ years didn't; crypto and
  stocks boomed. Shocks push people somewhere new - systems are connected.
- Critics are part of entertainment: "haters pay more than fans" in entertainment (engagement); in a PRODUCT they can kill it.
  Don't create conflict; let it happen on its own and take it all in.
