# Every system ships with a control room (Jaron, 2026-10-11 11:49 AM - "the vibe coder key")
From the Vex exchange: Jaron's real fear about AI-built systems isn't safety, it's that if the AI that built it is gone or out of
usage, he can't change it himself. The Control Room page (trading numbers, no code) already solved that for the desk.

THE PRINCIPLE: anything we build - game, trading system, website, show pipeline, app - comes with a plain-language control page:
what each knob does, what changes when you move it, what it was before (undo). Non-devs, vibe coders, and people who've never
used AI can run and adjust it without an AI or code. That's what makes our systems stand out, sold or open source.

First candidates:
- The Cosmos: a world/NPC control room (move a stuck or floating person, prices, job pay, spawn points) - his exact example.
- Heartbeat TV: queue/reorder episodes, pick next topics, approve/hide, without SQL.
- Desk: already exists (Control Room) - extend with the per-game cap, lanes, schedule in plain words.
- Twin setup product (money twins for others): ship WITH a control room - the sellable difference.

## Wider (Jaron 12:01 PM): not one control room per system - controls at every level (parts inside a system too), plus
VIEW-ONLY settings pages so people can understand how something is set even when they can't change it. Real-world devices
(robotics): open source yes, but we do NOT ship easy controls for unsafe changes (e.g. raising speed limits) - product liability
and safety law; check the legal side before building anything physical.
