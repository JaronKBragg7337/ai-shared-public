## 1. YouTube: AI-made educational channels in 2026

### What the July 2026 rule actually is

YouTube did not ban AI video. It did not add a new Partner Program (YPP) rule in July 2026. Matt Halprin, VP of Trust & Safety, said on Creator Insider that the underlying policy did not change; the old catch-all “inauthentic content” was split into three named buckets so creators could see the line.

Sources:

- YouTube Help, “YouTube channel monetization policies,” update dated 15 July 2025 for the rename, still the live page on 11 Oct 2026: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392 — **Verified**
- Creator Insider, “YouTube's Inauthentic Content Policy - Explained!” with Halprin, published 16 July 2026: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=14Vm0CiyUVE — **Verified (YouTube staff)**
- TechCrunch, Sarah Perez, 20 July 2026: https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/20/youtube-clarifies-policies-around-ai-slop-and-upsetting-videos/ — **Verified (press quoting Halprin)**
- Tubefilter, 13 July 2026, quoting YouTube’s example language: https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/07/13/youtube-inauthentic-content-monetization-policy-update/ — **Verified (press quoting Help Center)**

The three buckets that lose YPP money:

| Bucket | What YouTube says | Risk for Heartbeat |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Generic or repetitive | Looks templated. Little variation video to video. Slideshows, templated storylines, scrolling text, “AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal templates giving the impression of mass production without adding the creator’s original, authentic insights or perspective.” | High if every history/science episode uses one script shape, one voice, one visual layout |
| Unsatisfying or off-putting | Emotionally manipulative formulas, interchangeable copies of a format, shock for views. Animal-distress bait is the staff example. | Low for history/science/news if you skip bait |
| AI personas on sensitive topics | AI “experts” giving advice on health, legal issues, finances, or politics. Examples: AI doctor, AI podcast host giving financial tips, AI lawyer | Daily news must stay reporting and questions, not an AI host giving political or health advice |

Reviewers look at the **channel**, not one video: main theme, most-viewed, newest, biggest watch-time, titles/thumbnails/descriptions, About page. Too much of any bucket can take ads off the whole channel.

Halprin’s own test, 16 July 2026: AI that lets you make more high-quality work is welcome. The same tools used to stamp out cookie-cutter videos with no narrative arc are not.

### What is allowed (Help Center)

YouTube’s own allowed examples include:

- Using AI to visualize a unique character and narrative you invented
- Using AI to edit scripts or generate unique background visuals
- Series that follow a pattern **if** the substance of each video is materially varied and delivers creative or educational value

Faceless is not a disqualifier. An AI voice is not a disqualifier. An AI label is not a disqualifier. YouTube’s 27 May 2026 blog post: “a disclosure label alone does not change how a video is recommended or whether it’s eligible to earn money.” https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/improving-ai-labels-viewers-creators/ — **Verified**

Photorealistic or meaningfully altered realistic scenes **must** be disclosed. YouTube auto-labels if you skip it (rolled out May 2026). Animation, obviously unreal visuals, and AI-written scripts do not need that label.

Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491 — **Verified**

### YPP money gates (still in force as of 11 Oct 2026)

| Gate | Threshold | What it unlocks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fan funding | 500 subscribers + 3 public uploads in 90 days + 3,000 watch hours in 12 months **or** 3 million Shorts views in 90 days | Memberships, Super Thanks, Super Chat/Stickers, Shopping. Creator keeps ~70% of fan funding |
| Full ads | 1,000 subscribers + 4,000 watch hours in 12 months **or** 10 million Shorts views in 90 days | Watch-page ads (~55% of net), Shorts pool, Premium share |

Sources: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851 and https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13429240 — **Verified**

**Coming change:** from 1 Feb 2027, **new** YPP ad applicants need 8,000 qualified watch hours in 365 days or 20 million Shorts views in 90 days. Fan-funding gates stay at 500 / 3,000 hours. Already-in channels keep ads. Source: TeamYouTube, 10 Aug 2026, https://support.google.com/youtube/thread/451804019 — **Verified**

If Heartbeat can clear 1,000 / 4,000 before February 2027, do it.

### What makes an AI educational channel survive July 2026

From the Help Center + Halprin + the Chloe case:

1. **A thesis per episode.** One open question, one event, one argument. Not “10 facts about X” with the same beat sheet.
2. **Visible human taste.** Cuts, jokes, sourcing, a point of view a viewer can name after three videos.
3. **Variation.** Different structures, lengths, visual systems. A weekly “deep history” and a separate “open question” strand is safer than 30 clones.
4. **Stay off the sensitive-advice list.** No AI doctor, lawyer, money guru, or political pundit persona.
5. **Disclose photorealistic AI.** Label does not cut pay. Hiding it can.
6. **Do not read the news.** Original reporting, framing, and questions. Quote and comment; do not narrate a feed.
7. **Long-form for ads, Shorts for discovery.** Chloe’s money arrived when long-form held people.
8. **Spend enough that you cannot dump 10 near-copies a week.** Laramy’s $400–$1,070 per film is an expensive filter that also happens to match the policy.

## 2. X Original Content Rewards (OCR)

### Official rules (checked 11 Oct 2026)

Help Center: https://help.x.com/en/using-x/original-content-rewards — **Verified**  
@XCreators, 8 Sep 2026: applications open; last Revenue Sharing payout 11 Sep; first OCR payout for migrated creators 25 Sep. https://x.com/XCreators/status/2097371482804363669 — **Verified**

| Gate | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Who | Age 18+, Personal or Business account, not political/government, country on the list (US is on it) |
| Sub | Active **Premium, Premium+, or Premium Business**. Premium Basic does not qualify the creator |
| Followers | 500 **verified** followers |
| Impressions | 500,000 Home Timeline impressions from verified users in the last 90 days. **Replies excluded** |
| Pay | Qualified impressions on original posts: unique views by Premium users (Basic through Business) on Home Timeline, 50%+ of the post visible |
| Cadence | Every two weeks, **$30 minimum** |
| US payout | X Money (one person, one SSN, one X account) |
| Non-US | Stripe + ID check |
| Review | Meeting the numbers does not auto-admit you. Apply. One appeal, then 42 days before reapply |

X **does not publish a rate**. Anyone quoting a single RPM is guessing from their own dashboard.

### What counts as original (and what does not)

Counts: posts, Articles, video, images you wrote/filmed/designed/produced. Commentary, analysis, and adding context to news **do** count.

Does not count (no qualified impressions):

- Copied text/images/video
- Light edits (a word change, a filter, speed change, text overlay)
- Aggregations of other people’s posts
- Cross-platform reposts by someone other than the original creator
- Posts made with automated means
- Posts with a helpful Community Note
- Content “exclusively focused on monetization coaching”
- Engagement bait (“like and follow”)
- Undisclosed AI video of an armed conflict (90-day OCR suspension; repeat = permanent)

### What kinds of posts actually earn (from people posting dashboards)

Pattern across September–October 2026 payout posts:

- **Original text with a take**, often threads, in a niche Premium users already follow (tech build-in-public, culture contrast, founder notes).
- **Volume of original top-level posts**, then replies **under your own posts** (those replies do not count toward the 500k gate, but they keep the thread in the Home Timeline).
- **Images and native video you made.** GIFs from Giphy/Tenor do not count as original.
- Reply-guy farming is dead for eligibility. That was the point of dropping the old 5M organic bar and excluding replies.

### How this maps to Heartbeat on X

Earn:

- Native clips from the history/science shows, with a written take in the post (the take is the original part).
- Open-question threads: one unanswered science/history question, sourced, your framing.
- Making-of: how a scene was researched and where the model was wrong. That is original expertise.
- Daily news **commentary**, not a pasted wire brief.

Do not earn:

- Auto-posted YouTube links with no text.
- Aggregated headline dumps.
- Cross-posts of other people’s explainers.
- “How to monetize with AI” coaching.

## 4. Direct implications for Heartbeat Observatory

| Product | Do | Don’t |
| --- | --- | --- |
| History / science TV | Named series with a point of view. Claude for research and draft, human pass for sources and argument. Vary episode shape. Disclose photorealistic AI. Aim long-form (15–40 min) for ads, Shorts as trailers | Sleep-history factories. One template, new noun. Fake “professor” giving conclusions on politics or health |
| Open questions | One unresolved question per film. Show the disagreement. This is the opposite of generic | “Top 10 mysteries” slideshows |
| Daily AI news | Human-accountable host (even if the face is a house character). Original framing. Link sources. Think AI Explained, not a newsreader | Reading feeds. AI persona as political or market expert. That is a named demonetization category |
| Games | Sell on itch.io on day one with AI tag. Price the **inference cost** into the product. Keep a non-cloud fallback | Free live-AI demos that can bill $1,000/day |
| X | Clip + written take. Research threads. Making-of. Premium on the studio account from day one so the 90-day window starts | Auto-crosspost. Engagement bait. Monetization-how-to |

RPM reality for this niche: education/history is mid, not finance. Plan $3–$8 per 1,000 **monetized long-form** views until you have your own Analytics. A million history views is a few thousand dollars of ads, not $50k.

## Top 5 moves for a zero-follower start

Ranked by **speed to first dollar**, not by ceiling.

1. **Sell one finished thing this month (game on itch.io, or a $9–$29 research/teacher pack on Gumroad/Ko-fi).**  
   No follower gate. itch pays out from $5. A $2 first sale is documented in 2026 for an AI-built game. Put the Heartbeat name on the product so later YouTube/X traffic has somewhere to land. First dollar: days to a few weeks.

2. **Invoice the pipeline.**  
   Offer one Indiana school, museum, library, or local brand a 5–10 minute sourced history/science film at a fixed price. Same tools, paid before YPP exists. First dollar: one signed job.

3. **Publish 8–12 distinctive long-form episodes, disclose AI, and turn on a public tip jar.**  
   Ko-fi/PayPal from episode one. Super Thanks the day fan-funding YPP unlocks (500 subs / 3,000 hours). The episodes are also the YPP application library. First dollar: tips possibly in weeks; Super Thanks after the 500-sub gate (often months).

4. **Run X as original commentary, not as a YouTube RSS feed.**  
   Premium on day one. Daily original posts and native clips with a written take. Goal is 500 verified followers and 500k qualified impressions in 90 days, then apply. Budget **$200–$900 per two weeks** only after approval; until then it is marketing. First OCR dollar: typically 3–6 months.

5. **Push YouTube long-form to 1,000 / 4,000 hours before 1 Feb 2027.**  
   That is the last cheap on-ramp to ads. Chloe’s path: Shorts for reach, long-form for pay, high craft so the catalog does not look templated. Ads are the high ceiling and the slowest first dollar of this list (often 6–12 months from zero).

Do 1 and 2 in parallel this quarter. Use 3 to build the library those products sit under. Treat 4 and 5 as the 2027 base, not the 2026 rent.
