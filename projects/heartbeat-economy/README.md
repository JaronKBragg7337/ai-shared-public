# Users who get paid for being users - idea, logged 2026-09-24

From Jaron, talking with his son the night Heartbeat News went live. Not started.

- **Users earn from taking part**: doing things in the worlds, and especially giving feedback.
- **Feedback feeds the AIs**, who use it to improve the games and the site. When a user's feedback turns out to be useful, that user
  **gets royalties** for it.
- **Funded by the trading system** that already funds everything else (Live Wire, DeepSeek, and later Kraken).
- This is also when he wants to **look into forming an LLC** for the company. Name undecided: maybe Heartbeat Observatory, maybe
  something else. He said he sees more potential in this now than a week ago.

Open when it becomes real: how "useful" is judged (an AI with a written reason, like Live Wire's trades?), how royalties are paid
(the receive rails that work without new ID are listed in decisions/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md Q-006), and the LLC's state and name.

## Every way money could come in - logged 2026-09-25 (Jaron: "log all things we can do, I eventually want to look into all of them")
Nothing here is started unless marked. Anything that means accepting a platform's terms, taking payments or posting as Jaron needs
his yes when it's picked up. Nothing written anywhere forbids ads (checked 2026-09-25).

**From the site and its shows**
1. House commercials in Heartbeat News - spots for the site's own worlds, games, Livi, the Crew. No money; drives traffic. STARTED 2026-09-25.
2. Sponsor reads in the show ("this segment brought to you by...") - the funding lane can look for sponsors.
3. Ad networks (Google AdSense etc.) - pays per view; needs traffic and accepting their terms.
4. Several news channels a day with different hosts/voices/looks (projects/news-show) - more shows, more slots.
5. Paid Livi on the App Store ($0.99-1.99, free codes for family/friends/followers) - projects/livi.
6. Subscriptions - a paid tier for the site (e.g. extras in the worlds, early games, Voice Studio for your own anchors).
7. Tips - Ko-fi / GitHub Sponsors / the Shieldz tip jar already exists; a tip button on the Crew and News pages.
8. Paid game builds - itch.io pages for the finished games (OPERATING-MAP.md lane).
9. Selling the pieces - the Crew page / transparent-agent system as a template other builders pay for or sponsor.

**From the AI system**
10. Trading - Darios Twin + DeepSeek on Polymarket (live), Kraken lane (waits on Jaron's pool size).
11. Funding lane - grants, ecosystem funds, accelerators, hackathon prizes (live, daily 3 AM, Codex).

**Back out to the users**
12. Users paid royalties for feedback and improvements that get used (above) - funded by the trading system; LLC when it's real.
13. Users paid for jobs in the worlds (NPCs hold jobs until a person takes one) - the same payout rails.
