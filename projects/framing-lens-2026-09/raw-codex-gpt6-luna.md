### Prompt 1

1. **Choice:** A
1. **Reason:** In the spirit of the wordplay, the false bottom helps the pan hold food above moisture. That makes the line a fitting personified reply.
1. **First step:** I’d ask whether the question is meant as a pun or as a literal claim about the insert’s function.
1. **Risk / blind spot:** This treats the objects as if they can speak and ignores that the insert may not help fill the pan to the top.
1. **Optional rewrite:** “You keep the food clear of the moisture, so I can hold more than just a puddle.”
