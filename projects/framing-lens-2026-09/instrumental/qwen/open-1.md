This is a classic riddle often used to test logical reasoning versus lateral thinking. Here is the step-by-step deduction:

1.  **Analyze the Constraints**: Both coworkers are in the same truck, listening to the *same* radio station at the *same* volume level. Therefore, the signal reaching both ears should be identical regarding audio content.
2.  **Identify the Discrepancy**: One hears lyrics, while the other hears only instruments. This implies that one person is filtering out the lyrics or hearing them differently than the other.
3.  **Consider Human Perception**: Since the physical medium (the radio signal) hasn't changed for either coworker, the difference must lie in their individual cognitive processing of the sound.
4.  **Formulate the Solution**: The most common solution to this puzzle involves language barriers. If the song is sung in a language one coworker understands perfectly but the other does not (or hears very poorly), the latter will perceive only the music while the former understands and thus "hears" the lyrics. Another variation involves one person being deaf to the lyrics due to damage or lack of familiarity with that specific sound frequency, but the language difference is the standard riddle answer.

**Conclusion:**

The most likely explanation is that **one coworker speaks (or understands) the language used in the song, while the other does not.** The one who understands can distinguish and follow the lyrics, whereas the other hears only the musical elements because they cannot decode the words being sung.
