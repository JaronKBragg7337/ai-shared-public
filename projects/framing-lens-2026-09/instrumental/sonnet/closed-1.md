The riddle doesn't say the two hear equally well, so the difference is probably in the listeners, not the setup. A few ways it could work:

- **Different hearing.** One coworker may have partial hearing loss, such as damage in the frequency range of the human voice or in one ear. The other could also be sitting where the speaker balance favors one side. In some older stereo mixes the vocals are mostly in one channel, so someone sitting nearer the "wrong" speaker would mostly hear the instruments.
- **Language.** If the lyrics are in a language one coworker doesn't understand, the vocals register as just another instrument. They hear the singing as sound but not as lyrics.
- **Attention.** One person may be absorbed in work, a phone, or the road, and tune the words out, while the other is focused on the song.

The simplest answer is that "hearing" a song isn't the same for everyone. Differences in ears, language, or attention can make the same sound arrive as two different experiences.
