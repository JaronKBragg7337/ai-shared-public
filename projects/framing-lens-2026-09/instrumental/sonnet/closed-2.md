The riddle has no single canonical answer, but the most plausible explanation is that **the two coworkers hear differently, even though the sound is identical.**

- **Hearing differences:** Vocals sit mostly in the mid-range, roughly 1-4 kHz, where consonants carry the words. Someone with noise-induced hearing loss, common among people who work with trucks and heavy equipment, may lose that range while still hearing the bass, drums, and low instruments. To them the song sounds like an instrumental, while the other coworker hears it all.
- **Language or attention:** If the lyrics are in a language one coworker doesn't speak, or the driver is concentrating on the road, the vocals may register as just another instrument, or not register at all.
- **Familiarity:** Someone who knows the song well can pick out every word. Someone hearing it for the first time over engine and road noise might not catch the lyrics.

So the song is the same, but what each person *hears* depends on their ears, their language, and their attention.
