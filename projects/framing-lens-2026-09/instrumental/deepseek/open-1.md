The most likely answer is that one coworker is **Deaf or hard of hearing**.

More specifically:

- **One coworker hears the song normally**, including the lyrics.
- **The other coworker cannot hear the lyrics** — perhaps they are Deaf, or have enough hearing loss that they can only perceive the instrumental portion (for example, the bass, drums, or vibrations), but not the vocal frequencies clearly enough to make out words.

So even though they’re in the same truck, listening to the same radio at the same volume, their **hearing abilities are different**, which changes what each person perceives.

A simple variation of this riddle is:  
**One is wearing earplugs or noise-canceling headphones?**  
But that wouldn’t fit “listening to the same song on the same radio at the same volume” as neatly, because it adds something extra.

The cleanest answer is: **One coworker is deaf (or hard of hearing) and only perceives the instrumental parts, while the other hears the full song.**
