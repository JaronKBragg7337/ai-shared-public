One possible answer: **the second coworker is deaf** (or has severe hearing loss), so they don’t hear the lyrics—or anything—from the radio.

But if the puzzle intends that they still hear the instrumental music, then a better solution is:

**One coworker doesn’t understand the language of the lyrics.**

For example, the song could be in English, one coworker speaks English and hears the lyrics, while the other coworker doesn’t know English—so to them, the sung words are just sounds/vocals that blend into the music, and they effectively hear only the instrumental. They may notice a voice, but not perceive it as meaningful lyrics.

A common phrasing of this riddle points to: **the radio is playing a foreign-language song**, and only one coworker knows that language.
