**The riddle never says they're in the truck at the same time.**

The most likely explanation is that they share the truck on different shifts, and the song is an older stereo recording with the vocals mixed entirely into one channel and the instruments into the other. Some 1960s records, including early Beatles stereo mixes, were made this way.

When the first coworker listens, both speakers work, so they hear the whole song. When the second listens, the speaker carrying the vocals has blown, or the balance knob has been turned to one side. The radio, song, and volume are the same, but only the instrumental channel comes through.
