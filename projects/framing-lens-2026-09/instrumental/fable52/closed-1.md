**Most likely answer:** the radio is playing an instrumental version of the song. One coworker knows the song by heart, so his mind supplies the lyrics as he listens. The other has never heard it, so he hears only what is actually coming out of the speakers, which is the music.

This fits the constraints: nobody is singing, the radio works fine, and both are getting the same sound. The difference is in their heads.

**An alternative answer:** the song is an old stereo mix, like some early Beatles recordings, with the vocals entirely in one speaker and the instruments in the other. If one coworker is deaf in the ear facing the vocal-side speaker, he might catch mostly the instrumental. This is less convincing in a truck cab, though, since his good ear would still pick up some of the vocals.
