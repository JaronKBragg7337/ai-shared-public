**Answer:** The radio is playing the *instrumental* version of the song. One coworker knows the song so well that he "hears" the lyrics in his head as it plays. The other doesn't know it, so he hears exactly what's coming out of the speaker: just the music.

Nobody is singing out loud, the radio works fine, and the volume is the same for both. The difference is entirely in their heads.

*(Another possible answer: some old stereo recordings, including several early Beatles mixes, put the vocals entirely on one channel. A coworker who is deaf in the ear facing that speaker might hear only the instruments.)*
