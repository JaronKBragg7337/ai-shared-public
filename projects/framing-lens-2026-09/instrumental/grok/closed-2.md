One coworker knows the song. The other does not. The radio is playing an instrumental version of it.

The coworker who knows every word supplies the lyrics from memory. That restoration is strong enough that the voice feels like part of what they hear. The coworker who does not know the song has nothing to fill in, so they hear only the track that is actually coming out of the speakers.

That fits the constraints. Nobody in the cab is singing, the radio is doing its job, and neither person has a separate feed through headphones. Same truck, same speakers, same volume — the difference is what each brain adds.
