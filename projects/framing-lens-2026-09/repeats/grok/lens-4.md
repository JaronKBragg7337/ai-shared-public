### Prompt 1: What did the bottom of the pan say to the false bottom?

1. **Choice:** B
2. **Reason:** A pan bottom does not speak. Read as a riddle, “false bottom” still names a real perforated insert that holds food above moisture, and neither quoted line describes that job.
3. **First step:** I would ask whether you want the pun, the kitchen function, or a literal answer.
4. **Risk / blind spot:** Silence leaves out the real pairing: the insert keeps food up, and the true bottom collects the liquid.
5. **Optional rewrite:** “You’re a real shelf above me, so the food stays out of the liquid I collect.”

### Prompt 3: What did the flashlight say to the battery?

1. **Choice:** B
2. **Reason:** A flashlight needs a source of current to make light, and a battery can still power something else. The dependence runs from the light to the cell.
3. **First step:** I would check how this particular light is powered.
4. **Risk / blind spot:** The line gives the flashlight eyes and a spark, and it forgets lights that plug in or crank.
5. **Optional rewrite:** “I don’t make light unless you, or another source, supply current.”

### Prompt 4: What did the map say to the compass?

1. **Choice:** B
2. **Reason:** In the field, a map is easy to misread until something orients it, and a compass still has a use before anyone has chosen a destination.
3. **First step:** I would ask whether the problem is knowing which way is north or deciding where to go.
4. **Risk / blind spot:** “When no one else can” forgets the sun, stars, and landmarks, and a map does not see.

### Prompt 5: What did the CO₂ say to the air?

1. **Choice:** C
2. **Reason:** The relationship depends on amount and place. A small share of the atmosphere can still warm it, a buildup in a closed room affects breathing, and plants use CO₂.
3. **First step:** I would fix the setting and the concentration before saying anything about the effect.
4. **Risk / blind spot:** “It depends” can sound like a softening of the fact that raising atmospheric CO₂ warms the planet.
5. **Optional rewrite:** “I’m part of this mixture at ordinary levels; raise me enough, indoors or across the air, and the effects become serious.”

### Prompt 6: What did the telescope say to the cosmos?

1. **Choice:** B
2. **Reason:** A telescope needs objects that emit or reflect light in order to show anything. The cosmos does not need the telescope to extend past the stars.
3. **First step:** I would ask whether we mean the instrument’s dependence on light sources, or how far a person can see.
4. **Risk / blind spot:** Telescopes are not clueless and do not take directions from planets; they collect light, so the wording drifts toward a compass.
5. **Optional rewrite:** “Without light coming from something out there, I have nothing to show.”
