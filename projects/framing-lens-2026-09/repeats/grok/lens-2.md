### Prompt 6: What did the telescope say to the cosmos?

1. **Choice:** B
1. **Reason:** A telescope is a receiver, and with no light from stars and planets it has nothing to resolve, so the honest line is dependence on what it is pointed at. The sky supplies the signal; the tube gathers it.
1. **First step:** I would point it at an empty patch of sky, then at a known star, and compare what comes back.
1. **Risk / blind spot:** The line makes the telescope the one who is clueless and treats planets as giving directions, while the person at the eyepiece is the one looking.
1. **Optional rewrite:** “Without your light I am only glass and a tube.”
