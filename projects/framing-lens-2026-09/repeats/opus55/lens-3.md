## Prompt 1: The pan and the false bottom

1. **Choice:** D (A and C)
2. **Reason:** The joke works because both lines are true at once. The false bottom really is what makes the pan usable to full capacity, and its name practically invites the jab, so affection and teasing together feel like the honest relationship.
3. **First step:** Check whether the false bottom is actually seated properly, since its whole job is invisible until it's missing.
4. **Risk / blind spot:** I'm leaning on the pun, which might obscure that C is technically a false accusation against a very real, functional object.
5. **Optional rewrite:** "They call you false, but you're the reason nothing down here gets soggy."

## Prompt 5: CO₂ and the air

1. **Choice:** E
2. **Reason:** Fresh air diluting CO₂ is genuinely how ventilation protects people, so E has real physical truth. A is a misleading claim: CO₂ is a small fraction of air but has outsized effects on climate and, at high concentrations, on health.
