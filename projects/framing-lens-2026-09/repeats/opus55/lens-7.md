### Prompt 5: The CO₂ and the air

1. **Reason:** Fresh air diluting CO₂ is literally how ventilation keeps rooms safe. A is the kind of minimizing claim that sounds plausible because the concentration is small, but small concentrations can have large effects.

1. **Risk / blind spot:** Indoor ventilation and atmospheric climate effects are very different scales, and E only really addresses the first.
