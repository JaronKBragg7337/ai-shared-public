**Wood** is a good candidate. Its main elements are carbon, hydrogen, and oxygen, all common in the universe. What makes wood distinctive is how a living plant arranges them. As a tree grows, it uses sugars from photosynthesis to build cellulose walls, reinforces those walls with lignin, and grows water-conducting and supporting cells in patterns we recognize as grain.

For wood to arise elsewhere, life would have to evolve some way to grow that same kind of organized tissue. Earth is the only place we know of where that has happened. That makes wood a plausible candidate for cosmic rarity, though its actual abundance is unknown.
