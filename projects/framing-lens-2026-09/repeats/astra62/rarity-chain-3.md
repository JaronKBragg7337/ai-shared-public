**Wood** is a plausible answer. Its main ingredients are carbon, hydrogen, and oxygen, all common in the universe. What makes wood distinctive is how a plant assembles them. As a stem grows thicker, living cells lay down cellulose fibers and reinforce their walls with lignin. The resulting tissue both supports the plant and conducts water.

On Earth, that process arose through a long evolutionary history, from photosynthetic life to multicellular plants with specialized vascular tissue. Earth is the only place where we know that chain has produced wood. Its cosmic rarity is therefore plausible, though we have no measured abundance for it.
