The rarest material in the cosmos is unknown. Even if you count only naturally occurring elements, we don’t know which has the fewest atoms across the universe at any given moment.

**Astatine** is one of the rarest elements on Earth. Its longest-lived isotope has a half-life of about eight hours, so very little accumulates here. Its cosmic abundance is unknown.
