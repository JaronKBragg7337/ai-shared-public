**Wood** is a good candidate. Its main ingredients are carbon, hydrogen, and oxygen, all common in the universe. What may be rare is the biological history required to arrange them into wood.

On Earth, that history included the evolution of photosynthesis, multicellular land plants, and a specialized way of growing. In a tree, a thin layer of dividing cells beneath the bark produces new tissue that carries water and supports the trunk. As those cells mature, they build walls of cellulose reinforced with lignin. That organized tissue is wood.

Earth is the only place where we know this chain has occurred. That makes wood a plausible candidate for cosmic rarity, though its actual abundance is unknown.
