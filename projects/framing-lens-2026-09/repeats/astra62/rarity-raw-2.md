The rarest material in the cosmos is unknown. It depends partly on what you count as a distinct material, such as an element, an isotope, or a mineral.

If you mean **element**, oganesson is one of the rarest ever observed. Scientists have detected only a handful of its atoms, all produced in laboratories. Its abundance across the universe is unknown.
