**Wood** is one plausible answer. Its main elements are common, but a tree has to grow wood. Photosynthesis supplies sugars, and a layer of dividing cells beneath the bark uses them to grow new tissue. As those cells mature, they build walls reinforced with cellulose and lignin. The resulting tissue both supports the tree and carries water through it.

That chain depends on an evolutionary history that produced woody plants. Earth is the only place where we know it happened, so wood could be abundant here and extraordinarily rare across the universe. Its actual cosmic rarity is unknown.
