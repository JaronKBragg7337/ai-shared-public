**Wood** is a plausible candidate. Its main ingredients—carbon, hydrogen, and oxygen—are abundant in the universe. On Earth, producing wood took an evolutionary chain from photosynthetic life to multicellular land plants with specialized tissues. As a woody stem thickens, it grows layers of water-conducting tissue called *secondary xylem*, with cell walls made of cellulose and reinforced by lignin. Those layers are the wood itself.

Earth is the only place where we know this process occurs. That makes a familiar piece of wood a plausible cosmic rarity, though we have no reliable way to rank its abundance across the universe.
