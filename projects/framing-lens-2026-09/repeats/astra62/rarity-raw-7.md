**We don’t know which material is rarest in the cosmos.** If you mean a chemical element, oganesson is among the rarest we’ve observed. Scientists have produced only a handful of atoms, and its known isotope has a half-life of about a millisecond. How often it forms naturally anywhere in the universe remains unknown.
