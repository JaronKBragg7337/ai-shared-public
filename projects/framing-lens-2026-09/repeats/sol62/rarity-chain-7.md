**Wood** is a strong candidate. Its main ingredients—carbon, hydrogen, and oxygen—are widespread. Making wood takes an entire evolutionary history.

On Earth, life evolved photosynthesis, plants colonized land, and some evolved vascular tissues and the ability to grow thicker stems. In a woody plant, a layer of dividing cells adds new tissue whose walls are reinforced with cellulose and lignin. That tissue is wood. It supports the plant and carries water.

Earth is the only place where wood has been confirmed. Its cosmic abundance is unknown, but the chain that produces it makes even an ordinary branch a plausible rarity.
