There’s no known **rarest material in the cosmos**. We’d need a much better census of the universe, and the answer depends on what counts as a material.

- **If individual atoms count**, oganesson is an extreme example. It’s a short-lived element that scientists have made only a few atoms of in laboratories.
- **If you mean material in bulk**, antimatter is remarkably scarce in the observable universe. Individual antiparticles occur naturally, while large reservoirs of antimatter remain unconfirmed.
