There’s no confirmed rarest material in the cosmos. We can’t count every substance in the observable universe, and the answer depends on what counts as a material.

**Bulk antimatter** is a strong candidate for extreme rarity. Individual antiparticles occur naturally, but we’ve never confirmed a deposit of antimatter anywhere in the universe. If you mean a chemical element, **oganesson** is among the rarest we’ve observed. It’s been made atom by atom in laboratories, and each atom decays in less than a second.
