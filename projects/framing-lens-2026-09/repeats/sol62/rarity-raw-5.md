There’s no confirmed rarest material in the cosmos. We don’t have a cosmic inventory, and the answer depends on whether you count elements, individual isotopes, or substances that exist only briefly.

If you mean **the rarest naturally occurring element in Earth’s crust**, the usual answer is **astatine**. Its isotopes decay within hours, so only tiny traces exist at any given time.
