**Wood** is a strong candidate. Its main ingredients—carbon, hydrogen, and oxygen—are common in the universe. On Earth, making wood took a long evolutionary chain: photosynthetic life, complex multicellular organisms, and eventually land plants that build rigid, water-conducting tissue from cellulose and lignin.

Wood is abundant on Earth, the only planet where it has been confirmed. Its rarity across the universe is unknown.
