**Wood.** Its ingredients are common, but nature has to evolve something capable of making it. On Earth, that took a planet that sustained life over billions of years, the evolution of photosynthesis, and eventually plants whose cells could build tissue from cellulose fibers reinforced with lignin.

Earth is the only place where wood has been confirmed. That makes it a plausible candidate for extreme cosmic rarity, even though we can’t rank its abundance across the universe.
