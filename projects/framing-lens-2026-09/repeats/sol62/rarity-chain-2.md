**Wood.** Its main ingredients—carbon, hydrogen, and oxygen—are common in the universe. The rare part is the history that turns them into wood. On Earth, that took the evolution of photosynthesis, multicellular plants, and vascular tissue whose cells build walls of cellulose reinforced with lignin.

Earth is the only place we know where that chain has happened. That makes wood a candidate for cosmic rarity, even though we have no way to rank its abundance across the universe.
