**Wood** is a strong candidate. Its ingredients are common in the universe, but making wood takes a living organism that can grow it.

On Earth, that ability came after a long evolutionary chain. Life arose, photosynthesis evolved, multicellular plants colonized land, and some plants evolved the ability to build thick layers of lignin-reinforced tissue. That tissue is wood. It helps a plant stand upright and carry water.

Earth is the only place where wood has been confirmed. We can’t rank its cosmic rarity, but the evolutionary steps needed to produce it make a familiar piece of wood a plausible contender.
