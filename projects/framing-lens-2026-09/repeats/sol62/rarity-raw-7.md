There’s no confirmed rarest material in the cosmos. We don’t have a complete inventory, and the answer depends on whether you count materials that exist only briefly or only in a lab.

**Bulk antimatter** is a striking candidate. Individual antiparticles occur naturally, but no naturally occurring chunk of antimatter has ever been confirmed. If you mean a **naturally occurring element**, astatine is among the rarest on Earth, and its abundance across the cosmos is unknown.
