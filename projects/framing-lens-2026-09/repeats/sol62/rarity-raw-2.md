There’s no confirmed rarest material in the cosmos. We don’t have a cosmic census, and the answer depends on what counts as a material.

If individual atoms count, **oganesson** is a contender. It has been observed only a handful of times in laboratory experiments, and its atoms decay in less than a millisecond. If you mean bulk matter, **antimatter** is an example of something extraordinarily scarce in the observable universe, even though individual antiparticles occur naturally.
