**Wood** is a strong candidate. Its main elements—carbon, hydrogen, and oxygen—are widespread. Producing wood on Earth took the emergence of life, the evolution of photosynthesis and multicellular plants, and eventually the evolution of plants that build lignin-reinforced tissue to carry water and support their own weight.

Earth is the only place where wood has been confirmed. Its cosmic abundance is unknown, so the case for its rarity rests on the long biological history needed to produce it.
