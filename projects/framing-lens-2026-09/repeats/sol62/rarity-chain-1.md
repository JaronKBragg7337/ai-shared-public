**Wood** is a strong candidate. Its elements are common, but on Earth the chain that produces it runs from a habitable planet through the evolution of photosynthesis, multicellular land plants, and specialized tissues that build up layers of cellulose-rich, lignin-reinforced cells. The material itself is a structure assembled by living organisms.

Earth is the only place where wood has been confirmed. That makes it a plausible candidate for extreme rarity, though its abundance across the universe is unknown.
