There’s no single established **“rarest material in the cosmos.”** It depends on whether you mean an element, an isotope, a compound, or an exotic state of matter—and we haven’t inventoried the universe.

**Extremely short-lived isotopes of heavy elements** are plausible contenders: they’re difficult to produce and decay almost immediately, so very few atoms may exist at any moment.

You’ll often hear **astatine**, but that’s commonly cited as the rarest naturally occurring element **on Earth**, not a demonstrated winner across the cosmos.
