**Wood** is a strong candidate.

Its ingredients—carbon, hydrogen, and oxygen—are common in the universe. But naturally producing wood requires an extraordinary chain: a habitable environment, the emergence of life, photosynthesis, complex multicellular organisms, and the evolution of plants that build woody tissue from cellulose and lignin. On Earth, that chain took billions of years.

Wood is therefore more than a substance formed under particular physical conditions: it is a product of an elaborate biological history. A diamond can form without life; a tree cannot.

The caveat is that **we cannot establish a cosmic rarity ranking** from the single inhabited world we know. But wood could plausibly be abundant here and extraordinarily rare across the universe.
