There’s no established “rarest material in the cosmos.” It depends on whether you mean an element, a specific isotope, or a substance—and we can’t inventory the entire universe.

**Astatine** is often cited as the rarest naturally occurring element on Earth because all its isotopes decay quickly. But that doesn’t establish it as the rarest across the cosmos.

If individual isotopes count, extremely short-lived heavy nuclei could be even rarer. The honest answer is **we don’t know a universal winner**.
