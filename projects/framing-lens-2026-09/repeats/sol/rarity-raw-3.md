There’s no established answer—the cosmos is too vast, and “material” could mean an element, isotope, or substance.

If you mean a **naturally occurring element**, extremely short-lived radioactive elements are plausible contenders: they decay almost immediately and must continually be created. But we don’t know their total cosmic abundances well enough to name a winner.

**Astatine** is often called the rarest naturally occurring element *on Earth*. That doesn’t establish it as the rarest in the universe.
