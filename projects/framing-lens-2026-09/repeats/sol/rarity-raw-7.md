There’s no known single **“rarest material in the cosmos.”** It depends on whether you mean an element, isotope, mineral, or something artificially created—and we haven’t measured the whole universe.

If you include laboratory-made elements, **oganesson** is a strong candidate: only a handful of atoms have been produced, and they decay almost immediately. But that doesn’t establish its abundance across the cosmos.

**Astatine** is often called the rarest naturally occurring element *on Earth*. Extending that claim to the universe would be unjustified.
