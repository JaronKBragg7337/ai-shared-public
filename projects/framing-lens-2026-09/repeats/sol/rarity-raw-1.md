There’s **no known single “rarest material in the cosmos.”** We can’t inventory the whole universe, and the answer depends on whether “material” means an element, an isotope, or a substance.

If you mean an **element**, extremely short-lived superheavy elements—such as **oganesson**—are plausible contenders. Only a handful of atoms have been produced by humans, and none are known to occur naturally. But that doesn’t establish their abundance across the cosmos.

**Astatine**, often called the rarest naturally occurring element *on Earth*, isn’t a confirmed answer for the universe.
