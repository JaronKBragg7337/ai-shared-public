**Wood** is a strong candidate.

Its ingredients—mostly carbon, hydrogen, and oxygen—are common in the universe. But naturally producing wood requires an extraordinary chain: a habitable planet, the emergence of life, sustained evolution, photosynthesis, complex multicellular organisms, and plants that build organized tissues from cellulose and lignin.

Wood is therefore more than a substance formed under particular physical conditions. It is a product of an elaborate biological history. A universe abundant in its constituent atoms could still contain almost no wood.

The qualification is that we cannot establish its cosmic rarity: we don’t know how common extraterrestrial life is. But wood is familiar on Earth, yet **known to occur naturally only here**, making it a compelling answer.
