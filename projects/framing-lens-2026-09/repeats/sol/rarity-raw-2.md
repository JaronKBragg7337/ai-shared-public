There’s no known single “rarest material in the cosmos.” It depends on whether you mean an element, an isotope, or a particular form of matter—and we can’t inventory the whole universe.

**Astatine** is the familiar answer for the rarest naturally occurring element **on Earth**, because all its isotopes decay rapidly. That doesn’t establish it as the rarest across the cosmos.

Extremely short-lived isotopes or exotic forms of matter could be far rarer, but naming one as the absolute winner would go beyond what we know.
