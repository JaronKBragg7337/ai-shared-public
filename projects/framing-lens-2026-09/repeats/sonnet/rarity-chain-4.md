The best candidate is **liquid water**, or perhaps more precisely **water-rich, life-bearing Earth**, but the most interesting answer is probably **wood**, or more broadly **biologically produced materials**. Since you're asking about a single familiar material, I'd pick **wood**.

**Why wood is a strong candidate**

- **Ingredients are common.** Wood is mostly cellulose, hemicellulose, and lignin, which are built from carbon, hydrogen, and oxygen, three of the most abundant elements in the universe.
- **Temperature and pressure aren't the issue.** Wood forms at ordinary surface conditions.
- **The chain of conditions is extraordinary.** To get wood, you need:
  1. A rocky planet in a habitable zone with stable liquid water.
  2. An atmosphere with CO₂ and, over time, free oxygen.
  3. The origin of life, which is itself unexplained.
  4. Photosynthesis evolving, then multicellular plants.
  5. Plants colonizing land, then evolving vascular tissue.
  6. Evolution of lignin, a complex polymer that gives structural rigidity and allows trees to grow tall.
  7. Soil, nutrients, water cycles, and sunlight, all sustained over hundreds of millions of years.

Wood is therefore not merely a product of chemistry. It requires billions of years of evolutionary history on a planet with an unusual combination of properties. As far as we know, it exists only on Earth.

**Other contenders**

- **Limestone/chalk** and other biogenic minerals are also tied to life.
- **Amber** requires trees, then resin, then burial and fossilization over millions of years.
- **Pearl** or **silk** are similarly dependent on specific organisms.
- **Free molecular oxygen-rich air** is a planetary-scale product of life.

Wood stands out because it is so familiar and mundane, yet it depends on a rare planetary and evolutionary history. Other materials, such as diamond, form from rare but purely physical processes. Wood may be among the rarest materials in the universe because it requires life, evolution, and a long stable planetary environment all to occur together.
