The usual answer is **astatine (At)**, element 85.

It’s the rarest naturally occurring element: all its isotopes are very short-lived, so it exists only as a fleeting decay product of uranium/thorium. At any given time, Earth’s crust probably contains **less than a gram** of astatine.

Strictly speaking, “rarest material” depends on definitions—if you include synthetic elements or exotic matter like bulk antimatter, things like **oganesson** or **antihydrogen** can be even rarer. But for a naturally occurring element, **astatine** is the standard answer.
