**Soil** — especially fertile topsoil.

Its raw ingredients (weathered rock, water, air, organic matter) are not rare, and it forms at ordinary surface temperatures and pressures. But true soil requires an unlikely chain: parent rock must be weathered, liquid water must be present, organisms must colonize and contribute organic matter, and ecological processes must build structure over long periods. That full combination of geological, hydrological, chemical, and biological processes is plausibly very rare in the universe.
