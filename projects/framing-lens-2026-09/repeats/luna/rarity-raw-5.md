There isn’t a single well-defined “rarest material” in the cosmos. It depends on what counts as a material and whether you mean naturally occurring, stable, or merely present somewhere.

If you mean a naturally occurring chemical element, **astatine** is often called the rarest on Earth: it has no long-lived isotopes and exists only in tiny, short-lived amounts. But that doesn’t make it the rarest substance across the entire cosmos. Some exotic particles or short-lived forms of matter may be vastly scarcer, and we can’t reliably rank everything in the universe.
