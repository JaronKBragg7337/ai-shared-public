There’s no single well-defined answer: “rarest” depends on what counts as a material and how we measure abundance.

If you mean naturally occurring chemical elements, **astatine** is often called the rarest on Earth because it has no stable isotopes and decays quickly. Across the cosmos, though, it’s hard to name one rarest element: many short-lived elements exist only fleetingly, and we can’t measure the whole universe.

If you mean any kind of matter, exotic particles or phases could be far rarer still.
