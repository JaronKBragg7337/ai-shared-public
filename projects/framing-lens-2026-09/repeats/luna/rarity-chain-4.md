You could make a case for **wood**—especially living wood in a tree.

Its ingredients are ordinary, and it forms under no exotic pressure or temperature. But nature has to assemble a long chain: a suitable star and planet, stable liquid water, life, photosynthesis, and organisms that build durable, lignin-rich structures. That combination may be far rarer than the raw elements suggest.

The claim is necessarily speculative: we know Earth is the only place with wood, so we can’t estimate how rare it is across the universe.
