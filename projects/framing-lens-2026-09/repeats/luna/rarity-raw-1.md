There isn’t a single well-defined “rarest material” in the cosmos: rarity depends on what counts as a material and whether we mean an element, isotope, or substance.

If you mean naturally occurring **elements**, astatine is often called the rarest on Earth because it has no long-lived isotopes. Across the cosmos, though, we don’t know enough to name a universal rarest material; some short-lived isotopes or exotic forms of matter may be far rarer.
