**Living, metabolically active matter** is a strong candidate—especially organized ecosystems.

Its ingredients are common, and life does not depend on one narrow temperature or pressure. The rarity would come from the whole chain: a suitable world, long-term stability, accessible chemistry, the emergence of self-replicating systems, and the evolution of complex, sustained metabolism. We know that chain produced life on Earth; we do not yet know how often it happens elsewhere.

That makes it a plausible answer, not a demonstrated ranking: we have only one confirmed example.
