There’s no known single “rarest material in the cosmos.” The answer depends on what counts as a material and whether you mean the smallest total amount, the lowest concentration, or the rarest naturally occurring element.

Some unstable elements and isotopes may exist only fleetingly when they’re created in stars, explosions, or particle collisions. But we can’t inventory the entire universe well enough to say which is rarest. Even a substance made only once in a laboratory could qualify under a broad definition.
