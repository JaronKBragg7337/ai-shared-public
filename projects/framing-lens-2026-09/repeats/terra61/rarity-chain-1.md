**Wood** is a strong candidate.

Its ingredients—mostly carbon, hydrogen, and oxygen—are common, and wood can exist across a wide range of ordinary temperatures and pressures. But making it naturally appears to require a much longer chain: a habitable environment, the emergence of life, photosynthesis, the evolution of complex land plants, and the development of woody tissue.

We don’t know whether wood exists elsewhere or how its abundance compares with every other material. But as far as we currently know, nature produces it on only one planet, which makes the argument plausible.
