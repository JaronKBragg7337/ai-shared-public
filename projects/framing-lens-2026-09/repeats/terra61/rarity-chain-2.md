**Wood** is a strong candidate.

Its ingredients—mostly carbon, hydrogen, and oxygen—are common in the universe. But natural wood is made by trees and other woody plants, which require a long chain of events: a habitable environment, the emergence of life, photosynthesis, the evolution of complex multicellular organisms, and eventually plants capable of producing lignified tissue.

That makes wood a familiar material with an extraordinary natural history. We don’t know how common life or tree-like organisms are elsewhere, though, so its place among the universe’s rarest materials is an argument, not a measured fact.
