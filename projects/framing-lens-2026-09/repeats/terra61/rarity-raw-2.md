There’s no known single “rarest material in the cosmos.” We can’t inventory the entire universe, and the answer depends on what counts as a material: an element, an isotope, a mineral, or a particular arrangement of atoms.

If you mean a **naturally occurring element**, astatine and francium are often cited as exceptionally rare on Earth because their isotopes decay quickly. That doesn’t establish which is rarest across the cosmos.

If you include specific isotopes or unique mixtures, the question has no useful single answer: some may exist for only a moment, or in just one place.
