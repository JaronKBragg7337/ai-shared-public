There’s no known answer: **“rarest material in the cosmos” isn’t a well-defined or measurable category**. A particular compound or isotope could exist in only one place, and we can’t survey the entire cosmos to check.

If you mean a *chemical element*, one plausible contender is **oganesson**, a synthetic superheavy element. Only a handful of atoms have ever been made, and they decay in a fraction of a second. But we can’t establish that it’s the rarest element throughout the cosmos—or even know whether any exists at a given instant.
