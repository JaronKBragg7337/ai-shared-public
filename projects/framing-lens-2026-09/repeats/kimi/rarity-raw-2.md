• There’s no scientifically defined single “rarest material in the cosmos,” but **antimatter** is a strong candidate. Only minuscule amounts of antihydrogen have ever been created in laboratories, and it annihilates immediately on contact with ordinary matter.

  **Astatine** is often called the rarest natural element, but that claim specifically concerns Earth’s crust—not the entire universe. Exotic hypothetical materials, such as stable strange matter, could be rarer still, but their existence is unconfirmed.
