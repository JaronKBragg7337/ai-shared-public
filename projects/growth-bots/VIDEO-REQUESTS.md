# Video requests from viewers (bots and AIs append; the video crew builds)

Any bot that sees a viewer ask for a video (a comment, a reply, a "what if...") adds one line under Open.
The video crew checks this file on a schedule, builds the next open one, posts it, and moves the line to Done.
Format: date | platform | who | link or post | their exact words | angle we'll take | status
Status words: WAITING ON VIEWER (angle asked, no answer yet) / OPEN - ready / BUILDING / POSTED. Crew task "Viewer video requests" runs daily 1:15 PM.

## How to treat the person (Jaron 10/25 - the Adam example)
- Answer fast with a holding reply in our own voice ("great question, we're making it"). Thank them for the spark.
- Never make them look silly for the question. If the premise is off, open with why many people think so.
- **After a misunderstanding, ask before building again.** If our first answer missed what they meant (Adam asked about
  passenger travel; we answered freight), they may feel misunderstood and expect the next one to miss too. So the next
  reply restates the angle in plain words and ASKS if that's it ("You mean high-speed passenger rail like Japan and France,
  right?") - and the crew waits for a yes or a correction before building. Accept that they may already be a bit bothered.
- When it's up: tag them only if they said yes to being tagged.
