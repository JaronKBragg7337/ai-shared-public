## What @acsbambusa's TikTok Studio taught us (Jaron's screenshots 10/25)
- **Search is 77-79% of views**, For You only 16-20%. People FIND videos by typing questions ("how to make a game on your
  iPhone", "how to make a 3D game"). So captions and on-screen titles should be written as the exact question people search.
- **Evergreen how-to wins:** "Built a full tower defense game using only my iPhone and Gemini" (Nov 2025) = 9,104 views and
  still #1 in the last 28 days (524). Search keeps old answers alive; trends die in a day.
- **Best time:** viewers most active 5-6 PM, followers peak 4 PM (Fort Wayne time) -> schedule shows/clips 3:00-3:30 PM so
  they're settled when people arrive. Daily news keeps its morning slot (it's news).
- Series format (Day 1..7 as a cop: 26.1K, 16.3K) and 75% male audience.
