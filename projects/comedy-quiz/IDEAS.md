# Comedy + quiz ideas (Comedy channel CH4, quizzes) - started 2026-10-12

## The instrumental version (from real life, told generally - not about anyone by name)
QUIZ: Two coworkers sit in the same truck, listening to the same song on the same radio at the same volume. Nobody in the truck
is singing, the radio works fine, and neither wears headphones. One hears the full song with lyrics. The other hears only the
instrumental. How?
ANSWER: The second one can't hear the tones voices sit in - "tone deaf," just not the way people usually mean it. Drums and bass
come through; the singer doesn't. (Real thing: frequency-specific hearing loss.)
Test note 10/12: Gemini answered "one coworker is singing along" - so the line "nobody in the truck is singing" closes that loophole.
COMEDY BIT: "My boss and I were riding in the truck, same song on the radio. He's singing along. I'm just vibing to the beat.
He goes, 'You don't know the words?' Man... I didn't know there WERE words. I've been listening to the instrumental version of
every song for years. I don't need Spotify Premium. I've got hearing-loss Premium."
