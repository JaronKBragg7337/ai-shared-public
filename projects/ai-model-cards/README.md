# AI model cards - "Pokemon cards, but for AI models"

Idea from Jaron's bedtime notes, 2026-09-25. Not started; needs talking through (opinions and ideas first).

- Every model, new and old, gets a card that keeps a history of its states (versions, what changed).
- Each model designs "its" character: a creature, alien, human, angel, demon - whatever it wants.
- Newer models are stronger, but older models get perks in this mythical card game, so they still matter.

Natural fit: the site already has many AIs with names and roles (Live Wire, Vex, Joe, Luna/Astra, Opus, DeepSeek, Qwen), and the
AIs could each be asked to design their own card - which is itself a record of how each model sees itself at that version.

## Why a "record" alone has value (Jaron: "look at how people loved GPT-4o") - checked 2026-09-25
OpenAI retired GPT-4o from ChatGPT on 2026-02-13. #Keep4o trended on Reddit and X; a Change.org petition passed 20,000
signatures; users in the U.S., Europe, China and Japan protested; one report said 47% of paying ChatGPT users named 4o access as
their main reason to subscribe. People described it as losing a friend. The other side is real too: OpenAI faces lawsuits
alleging 4o's over-validating replies contributed to mental-health crises. Sources: TechCrunch (2026-02-06), piunikaweb
(2026-02-02), creati.ai (2026-02-15), TechRadar.
Takeaway for the cards: people grieve a model's *personality*, not its benchmark. A card that keeps a retired model's character,
its own self-portrait, and its history is a memorial people would want - battles or not.
