## 2026-10-03 11:42 PM - IDEA, NOT APPROVED: API AI as faction leaders (keep the door open)
- Possible: when no qualified human holds a faction's top seat, an API AI holds it and genuinely governs (cooperate vs
  compete, push to space vs stay home), instead of the scripted F5 stand-in.
- A human joining does NOT replace it: a human needs the normal achievements/respect and must be voted in. The AI can earn
  respect and votes and stay leader if people prefer it. Whoever loses can stay on as assistant/VP (human under AI, or AI
  under human).
- AI vote weight counts during the current season but never carries into the next; human vote weight/experience does.
- Funding idea: the Crew/trading desk earnings pay the API credits.
- **For builders now:** do not build it. Keep the architecture open: seat holders must be pluggable (human | scripted NPC |
  future AI agent), with a deputy/VP slot per top seat, and vote weight stored per holder TYPE so AI weight can be dropped at
  season roll.

## 2026-10-03 7:34 PM - one seamless Solar System; flying must feel great
- No loading screens, hidden jumps, lanes or frame-swap cuts: every world is in one shared space and you fly there
  continuously, watching the destination grow. (He saw the ship turn away before arrival and flashes into planet zones.)
- Flying yourself must feel good: an assisted point-and-go default, no artificial speed/climb caps, easy landing, an expert
  Newtonian toggle. Both NPC pilots and self-flying must be great.

## 2026-10-03 10:46 AM - Jaron's answers to the Bible v3 questions (these override BIBLE-v3.md)
- **Game 1 vs Game 2.** This game (Game 1) is semi sci-fi with real things where it counts (real Mars size, real shapes).
  Game 2 comes later with a team and money: exact real Earth, real-world progression. (Corrects the 9:12 AM entry.)
- **NPCs fill every role until a human takes it** - pilots, traders, a gas station worker, governors, faction leaders,
  whole factions. Humans take a role by showing up, earning it or being voted in. The game must be beatable solo and work
  with 1 or 100,000 players. Cooperation unlocks bigger things, never access: anyone can always fly to other worlds.
- **Mars is neutral:** nobody owns anything on Mars; its traders and staff stay NPCs.
- **Seasons (planned, never surprise wipes).** Length is a setting we can change (start with a default). Each season
  everyone starts again through Mars. Kept: home-world holdings, ships parked at places you own on your home world,
  reputation, and **vote weight** earned from last season's progress (so veterans keep standing, e.g. for president).
  Lost: off-world bases and stations. Jaron's own account votes too. Unplanned player behaviour (bullying, wars, alliances)
  is allowed; only nature (solar storms) forces change. Seasons can become their own story.
- **New opening (replaces the Mars crash):** you ride a transport to Mars with other huge ships flying beside you (shows space
  and ships at once), land normally at Mars, look at a screen describing each world and faction, pick one, board a transport
  to that world - and that ship crashes on your chosen world. You survive and build up to get back to space. Each season
  the crash has a different cause (solar storm, etc.) so replays feel different but still end in a wreck. The trailer
  will need a new cut later.
- **Reasons to go to space:** alien-looking ships that come close then speed away; home-world disasters (storms, meteors)
  that can force people to leave.
- **Earth is playable** with real continents and oceans (no real cities; players build where they want). Earth resources
  realistic; other worlds get sci-fi resources and fuels where real knowledge is thin. Real free data sources (with credit)
  wherever they exist.
- **Switching factions is allowed but leaves a mark:** others can label you traitor/pirate; licenses (ship, ground
  vehicle, digging machines) can be revoked, forcing black-market or other-faction routes; quest-given items can be
  removed; you can join the other faction on your world. Needs a full design (builders propose).
- **PvP anywhere except Mars.** Friendly fire inside your faction is penalized (reputation, licenses).
- **All faction worlds at launch.** No half game; factions and their planets come together.

## 2026-10-01 2:33 PM - money, saving, phones (he said "I just need something to be decided")

  replacement comes out of money you earned. Starting purse and wage numbers: decided by Claude in the builder notes (part of it
  ties to the opening he hasn't played yet).
- **Every world has its own currency**; credits (the bible's word) are the common unit, exchanged at ports. Mars has a local one.

- **Tower people become quest-givers** (unlock things, pay money).
- **The world persists and is the same for every player.** One ship, one set of holes, hires, bullet damage - all held by the
  server. A refresh puts you back where you were; no new ship spawns; a hired person is gone from the port for everyone.
- **Phones: every keyboard prompt (G and the rest) gets a touch button.**
- **Multiplayer test:** after space travel, or before - his call. Saving the world comes first either way.
- Elevators: the new default; stairs still fine in some cases.
