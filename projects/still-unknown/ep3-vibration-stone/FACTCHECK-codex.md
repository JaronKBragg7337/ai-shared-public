Overall: technically solid, but I found two clear errors and several claims needing tighter qualification. Most important: 18 kHz is not necessarily inaudible; comparing hand strokes with ultrasonic “hits” is invalid; the British-patent attribution is ambiguous; the 0.6 g levitation “record” is weakly sourced; and the proposed ultrasonic-cleaner test would not reproduce ultrasonic machining.
