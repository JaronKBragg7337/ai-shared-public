This is The Cosmos as it plays today, filmed in the browser with nothing staged.

You wake aboard the Ares liner coming in to Mars, with the convoy alongside, and watch the landing at Marineris Port from the lounge window. You step off at dusk. On the pads stand the ships you can fly: the Meridian, the Drayman, the Line Marshal, the Skiff and the Wayfarer, a small courier you can walk through from the stern ramp to the captain's chair. Then the Meridian lifts off, climbs out of the port's neutral air, and a Shrike raider comes at her. The fight view brackets it with its hull and shield and a lead mark, and we fire on the mark until its crew surrender. In another flight the Meridian climbs out of the air into space and flies free, with Mars under the windscreen. The last part is from the new intro: after a crash on Ceres you wake in the wreck, climb out and dig out the buried crate.

Every planet in the game is built at its real size, gravity and length of day.
