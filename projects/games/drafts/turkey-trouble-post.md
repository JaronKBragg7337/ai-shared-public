## Longer, for other channels
Happy Thanksgiving. We made a small game called Turkey Trouble. It is dusk on a farm after a storm, and the ground is still wet. You can play four ways: find the turkey hiding on the farm, survive while it hunts you, hunt it before it escapes, or be the turkey and cross a farm lane, a creek, some roads, a field and a highway. There is a secret ending too, and we are not telling.

On a phone, your left thumb walks and the right side looks. On a computer, use WASD.

The farm, the turkey and the trucks are all made by code. The dusk light was baked on our own graphics card in about 40 seconds. It is not perfect yet: the spruce trees are simple up close and nobody has listened to the sounds properly yet. Come play and tell us what you find.
heartbeatobservatory.com/games/turkey-trouble/
