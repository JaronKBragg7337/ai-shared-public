# Christmas game: the idea and what I expect (Fable, Thursday 11/26/2026)

**Status: ON HOLD since 11/26 9:38 AM. Nothing is built.** Jaron is sitting with this and may bring his own idea; no build until he says yes. Asked for by Jaron through Claude at

## The idea in one line
The same farm, four weeks later, deep in snow on Christmas Eve. Four ways to play and a secret ending, like Turkey Trouble.

## Working names (pick one, or give yours)
Reindeer Trouble · Home for Christmas · Lights Out on the Farm

## Why this one
- **It builds on what we proved today.** The farm, the bake, the people and the game rules are reused, so the work goes into the
  new look and the new parts instead of starting over.
- **It makes a series people can follow:** the same farm at every holiday (The Hedge = Halloween, Turkey Trouble = Thanksgiving,
  this = Christmas). The turkey is still around. It survived.
- **Snow at night with warm lights is the biggest "wow" for the least risk:** moonlit blue snow, coloured string lights that
  throw their colour on the snow, falling flakes, footprints that stay behind you.

## The four ways to play
1. **Light the Farm.** The storm tripped the breakers. Walk the farm with a lantern and switch the eight strings of lights back
   on before the kids wake up. Every switch really lights its corner of the world. When the last one comes on, the whole farm glows.
2. **The Turkey Remembers.** Carry the Christmas ham from the truck to the kitchen door. The turkey from Thanksgiving is still
   loose, and it has not forgotten. (Today's "It Hunts You", with a job to do.)
3. **Catch the Star.** The wind takes the star off the big tree and tumbles it across the snow. Catch it before it lands in the creek.
4. **Home for Christmas.** You are a young reindeer that slipped off the barn roof. Cross the frozen creek (you slide; thin ice
   cracks if you stand still), the plowed road (a snowplow, pickups), the drifted field and the highway, and get back to the
   sleigh before midnight. (The one Jaron loved, in snow.)

## The secret ending
The sleigh waits behind the spruce woods, at the Dummy's House. The reindeer knocks on the same door. My first thought is a
callback: "Knock knock." / "Who's there?" / "Not the turkey." But the Thanksgiving joke belonged to Jaron's son. **If one of his
kids has a Christmas line, it goes in exactly as they say it.** Santa is never shown: only a sleigh, harness bells and boot
prints in the snow.

## What is new to build
- The snow look: ground with footprints and sparkle, snow on roofs, fences and spruces, falling snow, breath clouds, ice on the creek.
- The night sky: the real starry, moonlit sky from Poly Haven that Claude already pulled today (not used yet).
- Coloured string lights, a decorated tree, wreaths, a snowman, a sled, a snowplow, the sleigh.
- A young reindeer, made by code like the turkey. It is the hardest new model.
- Mode 1 has new rules. Modes 2 to 4 are today's rules with new jobs (carry the ham, a wind-blown star, ice).
