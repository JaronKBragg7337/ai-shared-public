system into layers + pieces, match pieces across systems, drop to another layer when nothing connects). He finishes a
design, forgets it, then extracts its principles into the next thing. The work got recorded; how the principles came
together mostly didn't. With a record, AIs can see MANY principles per project and start finding their own connections.

"principle:", Claude logs an entry. Claude also logs connections it sees in his talk and marks them (claude-seen) so
he can confirm or drop them.

## His design process (10/26 7:20 PM, his words) - how the ledger gets USED
"Drop it all. Forget it all. Now remake it as one system with all new things... that's when you get your next product."
1. Extract several principles from each system. 2. Discard the systems, labels and uses. 3. Transpose with metaphors and
analogies (a principle can swap roles: input becomes output, constraint becomes mechanism, physical becomes informational).
4. Recombine into something none of the originals did. 5. Test: "until I test myself it's theory till all paths are
crossed out." Others' words = advice and paths taken.
So the ledger keeps three separate things per principle: where it was FOUND, what it SAYS (stripped of its project), and
what it GENERATED or could generate. Plus failed transformations (paths crossed out), so no AI repeats dead ends.
Preserve what was learned, not what was built.
