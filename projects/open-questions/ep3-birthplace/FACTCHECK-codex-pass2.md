UNSURE [s3] “the deepest split among living human populations began roughly three hundred fifty to two hundred sixty thousand years ago.” Correction: Schlebusch et al. estimated that divergence range from genetic models; “began” can imply a single, settled split date. Later analyses note other population-structure models. [Schlebusch et al., *Science* (2017)](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.aao6266); [review of African population history](https://doi.org/10.1093/hmg/ddab005).

UNSURE [s6] “A fossil needs fast burial.” Correction: rapid burial can improve preservation, but “needs” makes it sound universal. [Smithsonian overview of fossilization](https://naturalhistory.si.edu/education/teaching-resources/paleontology/fossilization).

UNSURE [s12] “volcanic ash at Rift sites has let many fossils be dated well.” Correction: ash layers can date or bracket fossil-bearing deposits; fossils are often dated indirectly, and the precision depends on the site’s stratigraphy and dating evidence. [Olduvai K–Ar dating paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/191478a0).
