# Science 8.6 "Phobos: the moon that's falling" - sources and what each claim rests on

The 3D scenes are a simulation built in Blender from published numbers (shape as an ellipsoid with procedural craters and
grooves, not a map of the real surface). The spiral-in and the ring are a **model**, labelled on screen. Every date and
timescale is labelled ESTIMATE / MODEL / PUBLISHED PLAN. Checked with web search 2026-10-14.

## What is simulated vs real
- Shape: ellipsoid with the published semi-axes plus procedural lumps, ~210 craters (Stickney and a second large crater placed by hand, the rest random) and three families of parallel grooves. Not a map. Grooves are drawn ~2x deeper than typical so they read at video resolution; said on screen.
- Mars: procedural ochre / dark-region / polar-cap texture, not a map; the thin atmosphere rim is a shader.
- Time-lapse and ring: a model with Phobos enlarged ~25x and rubble pieces enlarged; the ring sheet is illustrative. Labelled MODEL on screen.
- The Phobos surface shots use boulders scattered near the camera; real boulders are concentrated near Stickney.

## What changed after the Codex fact check
Codex (read-only, web search, 2026-10-14) verdict: "Mostly accurate and unusually well sourced" with 7 WRONG items, all fixed:
- Sol 24 h 37 min -> 24 h 39 min (narration n03 re-voiced, orbit diagram, this table). 24 h 37 min = rotation, kept only as "turns" in the WHY IT FALLS label.
- "26 x 22.6 x 18.3 km (NASA)" label -> "~27 x 22 x 18 km (NASA)".
- Hurford et al. 2016 journal: J. Geophys. Res. Planets, not Planetary and Space Science (diagram x2, end card, this table).
- Nayak & Asphaug 2016: a distinct re-impacting-debris mechanism, not "combining the ideas" (grooves diagram, end card).
- Paetzold 2025 leans toward accretion from a debris disk, not specifically a Mars impact (origin diagram).
- "captured = wet, impact = cooked nearly dry" too binary -> "captured material should keep more; impact debris was heated and should hold less" (origin diagram; narration n11 re-voiced the same way); isotope list now O, Cr, Ti.
- "In a few years, a few grams" -> "If the plan holds, in 2031 at least ten grams" (n12 re-voiced).
UNSURE items softened: baseball "off the moon for good" -> "right off the moon" (n02); "already cracked and loose inside" -> "probably loose and porous inside" (n06); "up to two sites" -> agencies differ, said on screen; NASA "<50 Myr" -> "~50 Myr".
