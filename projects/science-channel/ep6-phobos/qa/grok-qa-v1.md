# QA: science-ep6-phobos.mp4

**Verdict: fix first.** Do not promote.

Watched the full 6:24 episode with the soundtrack. Picture sampled throughout (2-second frames plus every scene cut); audio transcribed start to finish with word timestamps; loudness measured per second.

The script and most science labels are careful. The edit is not. Captions fight the graphics on every infographic, several 3D shots look broken, and a few spoken names are mangled.

---

## Narration cut off, words missing, or spoken wrong

Spoken lines are complete sentences. The voice track is not truncated. These are the word problems:

- **04:44–04:49** — “Martian Moon's e-exploration mission.” Should be “Martian Moons eXploration.” The TTS reads the X as a separate “e-.”
- **04:53–04:55** — Launch site comes out as “Tenga Shima.” It is Tanegashima.
- **05:05–05:09** — Rover name comes out as “Eidfix.” On-screen it is Idefix.

## Visuals that don’t match the narrator

- **00:14–00:26** — Voice is size, range, “closest moon… 6,000 kilometers above Mars.” Picture is a blurry crater wall. You cannot see the orbit or the scale.
- **00:28–00:38** — “Mars fills the sky… a wall more than 80 times wider than the full Moon.” Mars is a large disk, which is the right idea (~42°). Fine.
- **01:22–01:50** — Voice is the tidal bulge trailing Phobos and dragging it back. Picture is a static potato with no bulge, no lag, no torque.
- **02:00–02:28** — Voice is Roche-limit tearing (“pull harder on the near side… hold itself together”). Picture is a tiny blob on a white hoop. No stretching, no near-side/far-side.
- **05:20–05:42** — Overlay text: “Mars low over the horizon from a site near the limb.” Picture is a full Mars globe floating in the middle of the frame above a disconnected strip of ground. That also fights the earlier “Mars fills the sky” shot.

## Awkward silences, audio jumps, music vs voice

- No dead air in the middle. Music fills every pause.
- Chapter gaps of **2.7–4.1 s** of music only, at **00:26, 00:49, 01:19, 01:51, 02:27, 02:59, 03:26, 04:04, 04:38, 05:15, 05:42**. Same rhythm each time; a bit long, not a hard cut.
- Music bed sits about **6 dB** under the voice (speech mix ~−18 dB RMS, bed ~−24 dB). Voice wins, but the bed is close. It does not duck when she talks.
- True peaks ~−1.4 dBFS, integrated ~−16.1 LUFS. No clipping. No sudden level jumps except the fade in at 00:00 and fade out at 06:23.
- Opening **0.8 s** and closing **0.9 s** of near-silence are normal.

## 3D shots that look wrong

- **00:14–00:26** — Soft, smeared close-up. Specks float off the surface. Grooves are exaggerated (the overlay admits this).
- **00:28–00:48** — Phobos-surface slope. Ground is faceted. Rocks have almost no contact shadow; the big cluster on the right looks perched.
- **01:22–01:52** — Underside of Phobos is a hard dark cut, as if the mesh is clipped. Grooves look painted on.
- **01:54–02:28** — Orbit is a 2D white ellipse in front of Mars. It never goes behind the planet. Phobos is a speck.
- **02:30–03:00** — Breakup “rubble” is a sausage of identical blobs glued to that same ellipse, then a cartoon ring. Fine as a diagram; it does not look like a moon coming apart.
- **03:04–03:12** — Stickney. Boulders are spheres sitting on the crater, not in it.
- **03:14–03:28** — Grooves/craters punch through to black. The moon looks like Swiss cheese, holes to space.
- **05:18–05:42** — Grey terrain strip across the bottom, Mars globe floating above with a gap of space between them. Rocks on the strip look unplanted.
