| Water expands about 9 % when it freezes | Standard physics (ice density 0.917 vs water 1.00 g/cm3 = ~9 %) | confirmed |

## Codex fact check (2026-10-12, read-only, web search) - what changed
- WRONG "every road already has hairline cracks" -> "the small cracks that a road a few years old already has".
- WRONG "each swing works the crack open" -> "each one that catches water in the crack" (damage needs water in the crack; an air-temperature crossing alone is not a damaging cycle).
- WRONG "fire ... saplings come back stronger" -> species-dependent wording (sumac resprouts, young cottonwood often dies).
- WRONG author order: the 2023 paper is Gerber, Wilen, Dufresne & Style (PRL 131, 208201), not "Style et al." - fixed everywhere on screen.
- UNSURE items kept but softened: potholes "can open"; "pavement slows this down; the survey still expects poplar and aspen on paved ground"; the 20-year figure is framed as the design life and the faster decay as this simulation's assumption; the 50-year endpoint stays spoken as an estimate.
