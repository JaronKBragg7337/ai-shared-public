s04 (truck meets Pioneer 10, 685 frames, one continuous camera move). Deep space, the Sun a small intense star with Earth a faint blue dot ~2.5 deg from it (upper left early in the shot). Pioneer 10 at centre: white 2.74 m dish pointing at Earth/home, hexagonal gold-foil equipment bus behind it, two RTG booms with two finned RTGs each, the long thin magnetometer boom, struts; it slowly spins about the dish axis (3 deg/s, slowed from the real ~4.8 rpm). The HBN live truck glides in from the sunward side and parks beside it; its roof dish points the same way (home) the whole time. The camera then swings round to the sunlit front of the dish for "look at that dish" and ends on both dishes. Pioneer's layout and proportions follow PRODUCTION.md (dish 2.74 m, bus 71 cm sides, RTGs ~3 m out, magnetometer ~6.6 m); distances to the Sun/Earth and the truck's size are illustration. Hard sunlight with a weak cool fill so the shadow side is readable. No labels. Weak spots: the craft is backlit (darker) for the first half by design.
