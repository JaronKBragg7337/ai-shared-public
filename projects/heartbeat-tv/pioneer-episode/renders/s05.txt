s05 (slow orbit + labels, 1115 frames). One continuous ~180 deg orbit around Pioneer 10, starting on the sunlit dish front and ending behind the craft looking sunward; the craft spins slowly (3 deg/s, slowed for the eye). Labels are BURNED IN (overlay projected from the same camera plan, leader lines track the parts): "six-sided equipment box" (24-290), "RTG power units (4)" (105-610), banner "RTG glow: false-color heat view (illustration)" (135-600) while the four RTGs glow faint orange (false color), "magnetometer boom" (655-846), "gold plaque" (765-850), then from 862 cyan radio-wave rings leave the dish toward a faint blue dot: "radio signal home: 8 watts" (880-end), "Sun" and "Earth" (925-end). The truck sits parked in the background. Earth is drawn ~2.5 deg from the Sun (illustration; real separation at 40-70 AU is ~1 deg). Weak spots: the back half of the orbit is backlit, so the bus/dish back read darker; the rings are a stylised picture of a radio wave.
