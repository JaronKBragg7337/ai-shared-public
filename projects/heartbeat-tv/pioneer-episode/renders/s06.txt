Slow push in on the gold plaque mounted on Pioneer's antenna support struts (gold foil bus below it); the camera rides with the spacecraft's slow spin, so the stars wheel slowly behind. The engraving is an ORIGINAL simplified plaque-like drawing, not the real artwork: two simple line figures (one waving), a simplified spacecraft outline, a starburst of lines from a centre mark with a reference line, and a row of planet dots with a tiny probe path leaving the third planet. It is lit by a warm work light (illustration; in reality it sits in the dish's shadow). Weak spots: the figures are stick-figure simple; the plaque reads pale gold under the light.
