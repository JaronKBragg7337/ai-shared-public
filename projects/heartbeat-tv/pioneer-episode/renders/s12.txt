s12 (false-color heat view, 1066 frames). Side view of Pioneer in an "ironbow" false-color palette: RTGs hot yellow-orange, equipment box warm orange-red, dish cold purple/blue; dish faces left (toward the Sun). Glowing arrows stand for infrared heat: they leave the RTGs, some hit the back of the dish and bounce off away from the Sun (to the right), others leave the RTGs and the box's back directly; more leave the back (right) than the front (left). From 628 a green arrow on the spacecraft points sunward ("net push"). Burned-in labels: "false-color heat view (illustration)" banner at top for the whole scene; "toward the Sun" arrow top-left; "RTGs: hot" and "electronics box: warm" (30/60-627); "heat bounces off the back of the dish" (330-700); "net push: toward the Sun" (650-end); "about 63 W beamed one way, of ~2,000 W" (720-935, numbers from SOURCES.md). Illustration, not a thermal model: arrow counts and paths are a picture of the idea, and the craft does not spin in this shot.
