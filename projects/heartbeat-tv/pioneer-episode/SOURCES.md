## Where the brief's numbers are slightly off

1. "Heat from the RTGs bouncing off the back of the dish explains essentially all of it": the 2012 PRL thermal model gives about
   80%, with the electronics (~45%) a bigger share than the RTGs (~35%); the last ~20% is within the uncertainty.
2. "2011-2012: Turyshev, Toth and team recovered old telemetry": recovery began in 2004 and the Doppler set was complete by 2006;
   2011-2012 are the analysis papers.
3. 8.74 ± 1.33 is the 2002 PRD value; the 1998 PRL gave about 8.5 × 10⁻¹⁰ m/s².
4. Pioneer 11 last contact month differs by source (September vs November 1995).
5. "~2.5 kW thermal" matches the 2012 model value (2578 W); one SNAP-19 table gives ~525 W per unit (2.1 kW total).
