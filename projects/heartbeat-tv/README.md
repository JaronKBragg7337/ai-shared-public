# Heartbeat TV - channels you flip with a remote (idea, Jaron 2026-09-26 9:40 AM)

Its own thing - NOT the Grand Palace theater (the theater stays its own place; things from TV can play there too).
A page (maybe not 3D) that is a TV with a remote: change channels. Inspired by how Heartbeat News turned out.

## Channels (at least four, plus music)
1. **News** - Heartbeat News (exists).
2. **Movies** - including AI-made films: Three.js films, Grok/Gemini video, people's clips stitched into one long movie.
3. **Shows** - The Necklace Show is a show, not a game (it sits in /games/the-necklace-show/ because it was assumed to be a game;
   Jaron made it as a movie in Three.js). More shows to come - he keeps wanting to make shows instead of more games.
4. **Comedy.**
5. **Music** - a VEVO-style music channel.
- A show called **"AI Slop"**: people submit AI-made things (the stuff that gets called slop on X); after enough recommendations
  from others a piece moves up to the main channels and can play in the Grand Palace as a scheduled showing.
- **Coming soon (log):** scheduled showings in the Grand Palace.

## Why
Attention for Heartbeat. There is a crowd on X that embraces AI-made work and a crowd that hates it; a channel that shows it
openly, curated by people's recommendations, is a place for the first group and a talking point for both.

## Pieces that already exist
Heartbeat News (3D show, loops, commercials, Voice Studio), the Grand Palace shared screen + uploads, The Necklace Show build,
the theater's reel picker (/video/).
