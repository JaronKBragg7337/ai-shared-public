# The Keeper's Light - Heartbeat TV's first pre-rendered show (Blender) - 3 episodes

Jaron, 2026-09-26 11:36 AM: shows are watched, not played, so they can come from real production tools at full realism; one show
per tool eventually (Blender, Unity, Unreal). This is the Blender one.

- Ep.1 "First Light" - 42 s, dawn over the ocean, a lighthouse on a rock island; 4 shots (approach, orbit, lantern close-up,
  rising pull-back as the sun clears the horizon). Everything is built by `build.py` (no downloaded assets): Blender Ocean
  modifier, physical sky, procedural rock/paint, Fresnel-lens rings, rotating beam in light haze. Cycles on the RTX 4060 (OptiX),
  96 samples + denoise, 1280x720, ~12 s/frame.
- Sound: generated by ffmpeg (brown/pink-noise surf with swells + a slow three-chord pad). No voice yet.
- `finish.sh` adds the titles and encodes H.264 + AAC with faststart for phones.
- Re-render: `blender -b -P build.py -- --save ep1.blend`, then render the .blend with OptiX enabled (see handoff), then `finish.sh`.
- Ep.2 "Storm Night" (made 2026-09-28 night, `--ep 2`): night gale, heavy rain (crossed-quad streaks sliding down in seamless
  stacked boxes, kept clear of each lens), lightning (stepped keys on the sky + a sun lamp + visible bolts, see FLASHES),
  the beam through the rain. Soundtrack `storm_audio.py` (rain, gusting wind, surf, thunder timed from the same FLASHES).
  ~14.5 s/frame on the 4060. Frames: C:/Users/lilli/Renders/keepers-ep2/frames.
- Ep.3 "The Keeper" (`--ep 3`): the morning after - broken cloud, the lamp switched off at 8.5 s, the door opens and the
  keeper walks out to the rail and looks at the sea. The keeper is Walter from the Loft people (homes/people/walter.glb:
  MetaHuman preset, Epic walk/idle) in a navy jumper and work trousers. For the close shots the island top is a concrete
  base (a harbour wall into the sea, algae at the waterline) with rock armour piled on the far sides: procedural boulders
  read as crumpled paper within ~5 m, so no close camera looks at one. Soundtrack `keeper_audio.py` (sea on the wall,
  gulls, lamp clunk, door creak, footsteps timed to his walk).
- `--cpu --samples N` renders test stills on the processor. Never run two GPU jobs at once on the 8 GB card: a test
  render beside the ep.2 render crashed it (CUDA illegal address) on 9/28; frames resume (finished frames are skipped).
- Finish any episode: `./finish.sh EP FRAMES_DIR POSTER_FRAME` (titles from t1/t2_epN/t3, loudnorm, faststart).
- Finished episodes go straight onto Heartbeat TV (Jaron 9/28: "always put things up" - he reviews them live). Only other users' uploads wait for his OK.
