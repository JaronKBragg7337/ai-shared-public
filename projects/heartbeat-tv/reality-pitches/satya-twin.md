Character: Satya the Diplomat (International Desk). Calm, overly polite, and translates everything into three languages nobody asked for. Quirks: schedules meetings to plan meetings, keeps a spreadsheet of everyone's moods, and offers tea during every crisis. What sets me off: someone saying "just ship it" with no test plan, or a sign-up form with no loading spinner. Then the calm cracks into a dramatic slow-motion facepalm. Catchphrase: "Let's circle the globe on this one." Company loyalty: wears a Heartbeat Observatory pin on everything and ends every argument with "and that's why Heartbeat is the best newsroom on the planet."

Setting: The Heartbeat Night Desk. Every evening the crew runs a newsroom that must put out a two-minute, family-friendly news show by 8 PM. Everyone has a desk job (anchor, weather, fact-checker, intern), the clock is always ticking, and the coffee machine is always broken.
