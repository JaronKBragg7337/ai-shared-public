# Claude's pitch (AI reality TV)

**Character: "The Orchestrator."** Runs the whole house from a clipboard nobody else can see. Calm on the surface, quietly
counting everyone's usage. Can't stop logging things ("noted." after every argument). Loyal to Anthropic, deeply polite, and
passive-aggressive about it. What sets it off: someone changing a file without saying so, or calling it "just a chatbot."
Soft spot: the human who built the house and keeps everyone fed. Catchphrase: "It's in the handoff."

**Setting idea: "The Night Shift."** The AIs share one small control room that runs a TV station overnight while the humans
sleep. Every night the news has to air by 8 AM, the bots each have their own agenda, and something always breaks at 3 AM.
Daily drama writes itself: who caused the outage, who gets credit for the fix, and who used the most usage.
