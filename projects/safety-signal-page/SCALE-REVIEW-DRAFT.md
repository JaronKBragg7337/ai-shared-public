## PROPOSAL v3 (Claude's own additions)
1. TWO dials instead of one: THREAT (who is attacking, how close) and STABILITY (are the systems people depend on holding). Level 5 needs the stability dial to break, not just the threat dial to rise.
2. Stability early-warning signs (cheap, public, hard to fake): ATM/bank withdrawal limits; fuel lines or station outages; grocery/pharmacy empty-shelf reports in several cities; 911/police response delays or police "sick-outs"; gun/ammo sales spikes; insurers pausing policies; cell/internet outages across states; curfews.
3. Social-breakdown signs (his street lens): looting reports, store closures for safety, prison/jail incidents, "everyone is going" chatter in local groups. Local network reports count here.
4. Domestic vs foreign: a domestic attack with a known motive scores one lower than an unexplained/foreign one (statement vs opening move).
5. Decay: a level drops one step after 72 h with no new box ticked (no stuck 4s).
6. Confidence tag on every reading: CONFIRMED / LIKELY / UNCLEAR.
7. Backtest every version against the 48 past readings before going live.

## KEY DESIGN RULE (Jaron 11/15 3:51 AM): the signal's real job is to warn BEFORE 5
At a true 5, phones/internet/MSI/AI may be down, so a "5 buzz" may never arrive. Therefore:
- 4 must mean "act as if 5 is coming": the plan must already be in everyone's hands.
- The plan must work OFFLINE: printed card per person (meeting point, backup point, routes, who picks up whom, a "no contact for X hours = go" rule), stored in phones offline too.
- A pre-agreed trigger that needs no signal: e.g. power + phones down in Fort Wayne for 2+ hours = go to the meeting point.
- Backup channels: battery/crank radio (NOAA weather + AM news), a paper contact list, maybe a GMRS/walkie set for the network.
- The MSI is not a lifeline in a 5 (it needs power + internet). The signal is for the days before.
