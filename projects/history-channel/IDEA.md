## Ep1 fact check (Claude, 10/10 10 PM) - miles of line
Sources vary by definition (route miles vs total track). Ep1 used 23 (1830) / ~167,000 (1890) / ~254,000 (1916).
- 1830: 23 miles (also reported 39.8-40) - OK, sources differ.
- 1890: ~163,600 route miles (American Rails) vs ~200K track miles (NBER/FRED). "~167,000" is close to route miles but not exact;
  next render: say "about 164,000 miles of road" or label as approximate.
- 1916 peak: ~254,000 (American Rails) to ~264,000 route miles; ~395K total track (NBER). 254,000 is a published figure - OK.
Fix before YouTube: change 1890 to ~164,000 or add "about". Sources: transportgeography.org (miles of road), FRED A02133USA581NNBR.
