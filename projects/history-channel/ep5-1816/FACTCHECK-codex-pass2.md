## Problems

- **WRONG — s8**  
  **Quote:** “Byron proposed that each of them write a ghost story.”  
  **Correction:** This can read as including all five people named in the scene. In her 1831 account, Mary Shelley says there were four participants in Byron’s challenge; Claire Clairmont was present but is not counted among them. Say “Byron proposed that the four of them write a ghost story,” or identify the four. [Mary Shelley’s 1831 introduction](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/42324/42324-h/42324-h.htm)

- **UNSURE — s4**  
  **Quote:** “One review of reconstructions finds the average cooling over the Northern Hemisphere, in the year after the eruption, was roughly four tenths to eight tenths of a degree Celsius.”  
  **Correction:** Give the measure and baseline. Raible et al. describe roughly 0.4–0.8°C of annual cooling across the tropics and extratropical Northern Hemisphere relative to the preceding 30 years. Their review also notes that estimates of the 1816 Northern Hemisphere anomaly vary by reconstruction and reference period. Consider: “One review estimates annual cooling of roughly 0.4–0.8°C across the tropics and extratropical Northern Hemisphere, relative to the preceding 30 years.” [Raible et al., 2016](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/wcc.407/full)
