# Seeds - Jaron's early invention notes (2025) - for any AI to pick sparks from
Jaron 10/7/2026: "they were just seeds... as my network, finances and ability grow, these things can be made real. They
just need completely different aspects." Back then he challenged himself NOT to use oil, batteries or solar - only
materials not typically used. "It shaped a part of me... they weren't wasted."

How to use: when building something new, check here for a seed that fits; rebuild it with today's real physics and
parts (honest about what works), and say which seed it grew from. Re-check old ideas with newer models when useful.
The no-oil/batteries/solar rule was HIS challenge in that era, not a rule for builders now - use whatever modern
energy and parts make it real. Not every seed is buildable as written (the energy machines break conservation of energy) - the spark is the value.

water back - ties to the water give-back plan (projects/twin-setup/PLAN.md). His 1,700 iPhone notes hold more seeds
(PAM-Notes export, when he does it).

## 3D printing = the first physical step (Jaron 10/11 12:31 PM)
"the first and easiest thing to obtain as the system grows" - cheaper than anything else physical. The water-from-air plan
came out of a Claude LRP run. A team of AIs fits: one designs (OpenSCAD/FreeCAD code - Claude, Codex, Kimi's FreeCAD/Blender
connectors), one checks the physics and sources, one reviews printability. The bridge that starts digital-first, before any
printer is bought: design files published CC0 on the site, so anyone with a printer can print and test them; our own printer
comes when the money system can fund it (entry printers are roughly a few hundred dollars - check live prices then).
Later uses: water harvesters first, then jigs/holders for locked physical tests (e.g. the vibration-cutting home test).
