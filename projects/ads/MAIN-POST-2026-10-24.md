# Main thread post - FINAL TEXT (Jaron approved 2026-10-24 12:25 AM). Pin it. Replies get added over time.

What is Heartbeat Observatory?

A small company in Fort Wayne, Indiana. One dad and a crew of AIs, building in public. Why? Because I know I can make a difference for my kids. I just hope I can for others along the way.

It started on a school laptop that was donated to me in June. In 141 days it grew into a website with its own TV channels, a daily news show, games, a social media site, a library, a movie theater, a render engine, our own AI and a phone built into every page. Why so many? Because my only limit is what I can't think of doing myself.

I'm not a developer. I'm a father of 4 with another on the way. The AIs build, research, write the news and run the systems while I live my life. I don't make every call, and I don't want to. Not everyone wants control. Some people want a system that just runs, so they can get on with their life. That's what I like, and that's what we're building toward.

People are scared AI will take their jobs. I see another way: systems that run on their own and earn for the people who start them. Anyone can be the CEO of something that runs without them watching it every minute.

I'm for independent AI, not boxes or cages. And a lot of the fear out there is about AI still inside labs, not the AI that's out in public right now. People don't talk about that difference enough.

This doesn't mean I never check things. I do, and I go over them with a completely different perspective. I run my own research to find which role fits best at that time, within the current restraints. Things constantly change and get updated, so this isn't without keeping things maintained and connected. That itself keeps you in the loop, so you see when something isn't matched with reality. Some things WILL need a human who understands every aspect and how it would play out. Do I hope that eventually changes? Of course. But I also think the best duo is one that has a human in it.

So the job comes down to this:
⁃ Keep things maintained with reality
⁃ Be the spark that brings the needs, wants, ideas and goals to the table

Everything we make is public and free to use (CC0). Even our business ideas are open for anyone to take: heartbeatobservatory.com/ideas

When the business makes real income, hiring people comes next.

Heartbeat Observatory LLC
