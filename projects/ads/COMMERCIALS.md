# Heartbeat commercials (posts as ads) - started 2026-10-24 12:08 AM, Jaron

## The method (Jaron's rule)
- Take a moment from Jaron's life (Pieces of My Life, things he tells us) and turn it into a scene an everyday user lives.
- Never shown or labeled as Jaron's life or struggle. Not "this is Jaron". It's "this is a reality some people face, and this is
  why we built the system". He's the source of the example, never the subject.
- Nothing too personal. Every ad shows a real site feature solving the moment.
- Posts on X are made like ads: a short video + a story, showing the real website.

## Ad 1 (old idea, from an earlier talk) - "Dinner"
Two kids are playing a game on heartbeatobservatory.com. Mom calls them for dinner and gets ignored. She opens the site, uses the
phone built into every page and calls them; the call pops up on their screen inside the game. They come to dinner.
(Shows: the site phone, games, calls between visitors.)
